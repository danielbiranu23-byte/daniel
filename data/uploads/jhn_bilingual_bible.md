# Yohannis (John) — Afaan Oromo & English

## Boqonnaa 1 / Chapter 1

**1.** Jalqabatti dubbiitu ture; dubbichis Waaqa wajjin ture; dubbiin sunis Waaqa ture.
> *In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God.*
**2.** Innis jalqabatti Waaqa wajjin ture.
> *The same was in the beginning with God.*
**3.** Wanni hundinuu isaan taʼe; waan taʼe hunda keessaas wanni isa malee taʼe tokko iyyuu hin jiru.
> *All things were made through him. Without him, nothing was made that has been made.*
**4.** Jireenyi isa keessa ture; jireenyi sunis ifa namootaa ture.
> *In him was life, and the life was the light of men.*
**5.** Ifni sun dukkana keessatti ni ibsa; dukkanni garuu isa hin moʼanne.
> *The light shines in the darkness, and the darkness hasn’t overcome it.*
**6.** Nama Waaqa biraa ergame kan maqaan isaa Yohannis jedhamu tokkotu ture.
> *There came a man sent from God, whose name was John.*
**7.** Innis akka namni hundinuu karaa isaatiin amanuuf dhuga baatuu taʼee waaʼee ifa sanaa dhugaa baʼuuf dhufe.
> *The same came as a witness, that he might testify about the light, that all might believe through him.*
**8.** Waaʼee ifa sanaa dhugaa baʼuu dhufe malee inni mataan isaa ifa hin turre.
> *He was not the light, but was sent that he might testify about the light.*
**9.** Ifni dhugaa kan nama hundaaf ifa kennu sun gara addunyaa dhufaa ture.
> *The true light that enlightens everyone was coming into the world.*
**10.** Inni addunyaa keessa ture; addunyaan isaan uumame; addunyaan garuu isa hin beekne.
> *He was in the world, and the world was made through him, and the world didn’t recognize him.*
**11.** Innis gara saba isaa dhufe; sabni isaa garuu isa hin fudhanne.
> *He came to his own, and those who were his own didn’t receive him.*
**12.** Warra isa fudhatanii maqaa isaatti amananiif garuu akka ijoollee Waaqaa taʼaniif mirga kenne.
> *But as many as received him, to them he gave the right to become God’s children, to those who believe in his name:*
**13.** Isaanis Waaqa irraa dhalatan malee dhiiga irraa yookaan fedhii foonii irraa yookaan fedhii dhiiraa irraa hin dhalanne.
> *who were born, not of blood, nor of the will of the flesh, nor of the will of man, but of God.*
**14.** Dubbiin foon taʼee nu gidduu jiraate. Nus ulfina isaa, ulfina Ilma Tokkicha ayyaanaa fi dhugaadhaan guutamee Abbaa biraa dhufe sanaa argineerra.
> *The Word became flesh and lived among us. We saw his glory, such glory as of the only born Son of the Father, full of grace and truth.*
**15.** Yohannisis waaʼee isaa dhugaa baʼe. Sagalee isaas ol fudhatee akkana jedhe; “Inni ani, ‘Inni na duubaan dhufu na caala; inni na dura tureetii’ jedhee waaʼee isaa dubbadhe sun isa kana.”
> *John testified about him. He cried out, saying, “This was he of whom I said, ‘He who comes after me has surpassed me, for he was before me.’”*
**16.** Guutama isaa irraas hundi keenya ayyaana irratti ayyaana arganneerra.
> *From his fullness we all received grace upon grace.*
**17.** Seerri karaa Museetiin kennameetii; ayyaannii fi dhugaan garuu karaa Yesuus Kiristoosiitiin dhufe.
> *For the law was given through Moses. Grace and truth were realized through Jesus Christ.*
**18.** Eenyu iyyuu takkumaa Waaqa hin argine; garuu Ilma Tokkicha Abbaa bira jiru sanatu isa beeksise.
> *No one has seen God at any time. The only born Son, who is in the bosom of the Father, has declared him.*
**19.** Yommuu Yihuudoonni lubootaa fi Lewwota Yerusaalem Yohannisitti erganii, “Ati eenyu?” jedhanii gaafachiisanitti dhuga baʼumsi Yohannis kenne kana ture.
> *This is John’s testimony, when the Jews sent priests and Levites from Jerusalem to ask him, “Who are you?”*
**20.** Inni dhugaa baʼe malee hin ganne; “Ani Kiristoos miti” jedhee dhugaa baʼe.
> *He declared, and didn’t deny, but he declared, “I am not the Christ.”*
**21.** Isaanis, “Yoos ati eenyu ree? Eeliyaas moo?” jedhanii isa gaafatan. Innis, “Lakki, ani isa miti” jedhee deebiseef. Isaanis, “Yoos ati Raajichaa ree?” jedhaniin. Inni immoo, “Lakki” jedheen.
> *They asked him, “What then? Are you Elijah?” He said, “I am not.” “Are you the prophet?” He answered, “No.”*
**22.** Isaanis, “Ati eenyu ree? Akka warra nu erganiif deebii kenninuuf ati eenyu ofiin jetta?” jedhaniin.
> *They said therefore to him, “Who are you? Give us an answer to take back to those who sent us. What do you say about yourself?”*
**23.** Yohannisis dubbii Isaayyaas raajichi dubbate sanaan deebisee, “Ani sagalee gammoojjii keessaa, ‘Gooftaaf karaa qajeelchaa’ jedhee iyyu sanaa dha” jedheen.
> *He said, “I am the voice of one crying in the wilderness, ‘Make straight the way of the Lord,’ as Isaiah the prophet said.”*
**24.** Fariisonni ergaman tokko tokkos,
> *The ones who had been sent were from the Pharisees.*
**25.** akkana jedhanii isa gaafatan; “Ati erga Kiristoos yookaan Eeliyaas yookaan raajicha taʼuu baattee maaliif cuuphxa ree?”
> *They asked him, “Why then do you baptize if you are not the Christ, nor Elijah, nor the prophet?”*
**26.** Yohannis immoo akkana jedhee deebiseef; “Ani bishaaniin nan cuupha; namni isin isa hin beekne tokko garuu isin gidduu dhaabata.
> *John answered them, “I baptize in water, but among you stands one whom you don’t know.*
**27.** Innis isa na duubaan dhufu kan ani hidhaa kophee isaa iyyuu hiikuufii hin mallee dha.”
> *He is the one who comes after me, who is preferred before me, whose sandal strap I’m not worthy to loosen.”*
**28.** Wanni kun hundi Yordaanos gama iddoo Yohannis itti cuuphaa ture Biitaaniyaa keessatti taʼe.
> *These things were done in Bethany beyond the Jordan, where John was baptizing.*
**29.** Guyyaa itti aanutti Yohannis utuu Yesuus gara isaa dhufuu argee akkana jedhe; “Ilaa Hoolaa Waaqaa isa cubbuu addunyaa balleessu!
> *The next day, he saw Jesus coming to him, and said, “Behold, the Lamb of God, who takes away the sin of the world!*
**30.** Inni ani, ‘Namni na duubaan dhufu na caala; inni na dura tureetii’ jedhee waaʼee isaa dubbadhe sun isa kana.
> *This is he of whom I said, ‘After me comes a man who is preferred before me, for he was before me.’*
**31.** Ani mataan koo iyyuu isa hin beekun ture; sababiin ani bishaaniin cuuphaa dhufeef garuu akka inni saba Israaʼelitti mulʼifamuuf.”
> *I didn’t know him, but for this reason I came baptizing in water, that he would be revealed to Israel.”*
**32.** Yohannis itti fufee akkana jedhee dhugaa baʼe; “Ani utuu Hafuurri akkuma gugeetti samii irraa gad buʼee isa irra jiraatuu nan arge.
> *John testified, saying, “I have seen the Spirit descending like a dove out of heaven, and it remained on him.*
**33.** Inni akka ani bishaaniin cuuphuuf na erge sun, ‘Namichi ati utuu Hafuurri gad buʼee isa irra jiraatuu argitu sun, isa Hafuura Qulqulluudhaan cuuphu dha’ jedhee utuu natti himuu baatee silaa ani iyyuu isa hin beeku ture.
> *I didn’t recognize him, but he who sent me to baptize in water said to me, ‘On whomever you will see the Spirit descending and remaining on him is he who baptizes in the Holy Spirit.’*
**34.** Anis argeera; akka inni Ilma Waaqaa taʼes dhugaa baʼeera.”
> *I have seen and have testified that this is the Son of God.”*
**35.** Guyyaa itti aanutti Yohannis ammas barattoota isaa keessaa lama wajjin achi ture.
> *Again, the next day, John was standing with two of his disciples,*
**36.** Innis utuu Yesuus achi irra darbuu argee, “Ilaa, Hoolaa Waaqaa!” jedhe.
> *and he looked at Jesus as he walked, and said, “Behold, the Lamb of God!”*
**37.** Barattoonni lamaanis waan kana dubbachuu isaa dhagaʼanii Yesuus duukaa buʼan.
> *The two disciples heard him speak, and they followed Jesus.*
**38.** Yesuusis of irra garagalee isa duukaa buʼuu isaanii argee, “Isin maal barbaaddu?” jedheen. Isaanis, “Rabbii, ati eessa jiraatta?” jedhaniin. Rabbii jechuun Barsiisaa jechuu dha.
> *Jesus turned and saw them following, and said to them, “What are you looking for?” They said to him, “Rabbi” (which is to say, being interpreted, Teacher), “where are you staying?”*
**39.** Innis, “Kottaatii ilaalaa” jedheen. Isaanis dhaqanii akka inni eessa jiraatu argan; gaafa sana isa wajjin oolan. Yeroon sunis gara saʼaatii kurnaffaa ture.
> *He said to them, “Come and see.” They came and saw where he was staying, and they stayed with him that day. It was about the tenth hour.*
**40.** Warra waan Yohannis dubbate dhagaʼanii Yesuus duukaa buʼan lamaan keessaa inni tokko Indiriiyaas obboleessa Simoon Phexros ture.
> *One of the two who heard John and followed him was Andrew, Simon Peter’s brother.*
**41.** Innis duraan dursee obboleessa isaa Simoonin argatee, “Nu Masiihicha arganneerra” jedheen; Masiihii jechuun Kiristoos jechuu dha.
> *He first found his own brother, Simon, and said to him, “We have found the Messiah!” (which is, being interpreted, Christ ).*
**42.** Indiriiyaasis Simʼoonin gara Yesuusitti fide. Yesuusis isa ilaalee, “Ati Simoon ilma Yohannis, Keefaa jedhamtee ni waamamta” jedheen. Keefaa jechuun Phexros jechuu dha.
> *He brought him to Jesus. Jesus looked at him and said, “You are Simon the son of Jonah. You shall be called Cephas” (which is by interpretation, Peter).*
**43.** Yesuusis guyyaa itti aanutti Galiilaa dhaquu barbaade. Innis Fiiliphoosin argee, “Na duukaa buʼi” jedheen.
> *On the next day, he was determined to go out into Galilee, and he found Philip. Jesus said to him, “Follow me.”*
**44.** Fiiliphoosis akkuma Indiriiyaasii fi Phexros nama magaalaa Beetisayidaa ture.
> *Now Philip was from Bethsaida, the city of Andrew and Peter.*
**45.** Fiiliphoos Naatnaaʼelin argee, “Isa Museen kitaaba Seeraa keessatti waaʼee isaa barreessee fi isa raajonnis waaʼee isaa barreessan, ilma Yoosef, Yesuus nama Naazreeti arganneerra” jedheen.
> *Philip found Nathanael, and said to him, “We have found him of whom Moses in the law and also the prophets, wrote: Jesus of Nazareth, the son of Joseph.”*
**46.** Naatnaaʼelis, “Naazreeti keessaa wanni gaariin baʼuu ni dandaʼaa?” jedhee gaafate. Fiiliphoosis, “Kottuutii ilaali” jedheen.
> *Nathanael said to him, “Can any good thing come out of Nazareth?” Philip said to him, “Come and see.”*
**47.** Yesuusis utuu Naatnaaʼel gara isaa dhufuu argee, “Ilaa Israaʼelicha dhugaa kan haxxummaan keessa hin jirre!” jedhee waaʼee isaa dubbate.
> *Jesus saw Nathanael coming to him, and said about him, “Behold, an Israelite indeed, in whom is no deceit!”*
**48.** Naatnaaʼelis, “Ati eessatti na beekta?” jedheen. Yesuusis, “Utuu Fiiliphoos si hin waamin dura yeroo ati muka harbuu jala turtettin si arge” jedhee deebiseef.
> *Nathanael said to him, “How do you know me?” Jesus answered him, “Before Philip called you, when you were under the fig tree, I saw you.”*
**49.** Naatnaaʼelis, “Yaa Barsiisaa, ati Ilma Waaqaa ti! Ati Mootii Israaʼel” jedheen.
> *Nathanael answered him, “Rabbi, you are the Son of God! You are King of Israel!”*
**50.** Yesuus immoo akkana jedheen; “Ati sababii ani muka harbuu jalattan si arge siin jedheef amantee? Waan kana caalu iyyuu arguuf jirta.”
> *Jesus answered him, “Because I told you, ‘I saw you underneath the fig tree,’ do you believe? You will see greater things than these!”*
**51.** Innis itti dabalee, “Ani dhuguman, dhuguman isiniin jedha; samiin banamee utuu ergamoonni Waaqaa Ilma Namaa irra ol baʼanii gad buʼanuu ni argitu” jedheen.
> *He said to him, “Most certainly, I tell you all, hereafter you will see heaven opened, and the angels of God ascending and descending on the Son of Man.”*

## Boqonnaa 2 / Chapter 2

**1.** Guyyaa sadaffaatti Qaanaa Galiilaa keessa cidha gaaʼelaatu ture. Haati Yesuusiis achi turte;
> *The third day, there was a wedding in Cana of Galilee. Jesus’ mother was there.*
**2.** Yesuusii fi barattoonni isaa gara cidha sanaatti waamamanii turan.
> *Jesus also was invited, with his disciples, to the wedding.*
**3.** Yommuu Daadhiin wayinii dhumetti haati Yesuus, “Jarri daadhii wayinii hin qaban” isaan jette.
> *When the wine ran out, Jesus’ mother said to him, “They have no wine.”*
**4.** Yesuus immoo deebisee, “Dubartii nana! Maal godhi naan jetta? Yeroon koo amma illee hin geenye” jedheen.
> *Jesus said to her, “Woman, what does that have to do with you and me? My hour has not yet come.”*
**5.** Haati isaas tajaajiltootaan, “Waanuma inni isinitti himu hunda godhaa” jette.
> *His mother said to the servants, “Whatever he says to you, do it.”*
**6.** Gaaniiwwan dhagaa jaʼa sirna qulqulleessuu Yihuudootaatiif achi dhaabamanii turan; tokkoon tokkoon isaaniis gara liitiroo 75 hamma 115 qabata ture.
> *Now there were six water pots of stone set there after the Jews’ way of purifying, containing two or three metretes apiece.*
**7.** Yesuus tajaajiltootaan, “Gaaniiwwanitti bishaan guutaa” jedhe; isaanis hamma afaan gaaniiwwaniitti guutan.
> *Jesus said to them, “Fill the water pots with water.” So they filled them up to the brim.*
**8.** Innis, “Egaa budduuqsaatii abbaa cidhaatti kennaa” jedheen. Isaanis akkasuma godhan;
> *He said to them, “Now draw some out, and take it to the ruler of the feast.” So they took it.*
**9.** abbaan cidhaas bishaan gara daadhii wayiniitti geeddarame sana afaaniin qabee ilaale. Daadhiin wayinii sun eessaa akka dhufe tajaajiltoota bishaan waraabanitu beeka malee abbaan cidhaa sun hin beeku ture. Innis misirricha waamee
> *When the ruler of the feast tasted the water now become wine, and didn’t know where it came from (but the servants who had drawn the water knew), the ruler of the feast called the bridegroom*
**10.** akkana jedheen; “Namni hundi daadhii wayinii isa gaarii jalqabatti dhiʼeessee, erga keessumoonni machaaʼanii booddee immoo daadhii isa laafaa dhiʼeessa; ati garuu daadhii gaarii hamma ammaatti tursite.”
> *and said to him, “Everyone serves the good wine first, and when the guests have drunk freely, then that which is worse. You have kept the good wine until now!”*
**11.** Yesuus mallattoowwan isaa keessaa isa jalqabaa kana Qaanaa Galiilaa keessatti hojjete. Ulfina isaas ni mulʼise; barattoonni isaas isatti amanan.
> *This beginning of his signs Jesus did in Cana of Galilee, and revealed his glory; and his disciples believed in him.*
**12.** Kana booddees inni haadha isaa, obboloota isaatii fi barattoota isaa wajjin Qifirnaahomitti gad buʼe. Isaanis guyyoota muraasa achi turan.
> *After this, he went down to Capernaum, he, and his mother, his brothers, and his disciples; and they stayed there a few days.*
**13.** Yesuusis waan Faasiikaan Yihuudootaa dhiʼaateef Yerusaalemitti ol baʼe.
> *The Passover of the Jews was at hand, and Jesus went up to Jerusalem.*
**14.** Mana qulqullummaa keessattis warra loon, hoolotaa fi gugee gurguratanii fi warra taaʼanii maallaqa geeddaran arge.
> *He found in the temple those who sold oxen, sheep, and doves, and the changers of money sitting.*
**15.** Innis quncee irraa qaccee dhaʼatee nama hundumaa hoolotaa fi loon wajjin mana qulqullummaa keessaa ariʼe; saantima warra maallaqa geeddaraniis bittinneesse; minjaala isaaniis garagalche.
> *He made a whip of cords and drove all out of the temple, both the sheep and the oxen; and he poured out the changers’ money and overthrew their tables.*
**16.** Warra gugee gurguraniinis, “Kana hunda asii baasaa! Mana Abbaa koo mana daldalaa hin godhinaa!” jedhe.
> *To those who sold the doves, he said, “Take these things out of here! Don’t make my Father’s house a marketplace!”*
**17.** Barattoonni isaas, “Hinaaffaan mana keetii na gugguba” jedhamee akka barreeffame yaadatan.
> *His disciples remembered that it was written, “Zeal for your house will eat me up.”*
**18.** Yihuudoonnis deebisanii, “Kana hunda hojjechuuf akka taayitaa qabdu mirkaneessuuf mallattoo maalii nutti argisiisuu dandeessa?” jedhanii isa gaafatan.
> *The Jews therefore answered him, “What sign do you show us, seeing that you do these things?”*
**19.** Yesuus, “Mana qulqullummaa kana jigsaa; ani immoo guyyaa sadiitti olin dhaabaa” jedhee deebiseef.
> *Jesus answered them, “Destroy this temple, and in three days I will raise it up.”*
**20.** Yihuudoonnis deebisanii, “Mana qulqullummaa kana ijaaruun waggaa afurtamii jaʼa fudhate; ati immoo guyyaa sadii keessatti ol dhaabdaa?” jedhaniin.
> *The Jews therefore said, “It took forty-six years to build this temple! Will you raise it up in three days?”*
**21.** Manni qulqullummaa kan inni waaʼee isaa dubbate sun garuu dhagna isaa ti.
> *But he spoke of the temple of his body.*
**22.** Erga inni duʼaa kaʼee booddee barattoonni isaa waan inni dubbatee ture sana yaadatan. Isaanis Katabbii Qulqulluu fi dubbii Yesuus dubbate amanan.
> *When therefore he was raised from the dead, his disciples remembered that he said this, and they believed the Scripture and the word which Jesus had said.*
**23.** Yeroo inni Ayyaana Faasiikaatiif jedhee Yerusaalem turetti namoonni baayʼeen mallattoo inni hojjete arganii maqaa isaatti amanan.
> *Now when he was in Jerusalem at the Passover, during the feast, many believed in his name, observing his signs which he did.*
**24.** Yesuus garuu waan nama hunda beekuuf, amanee isaanitti of hin dhiifne.
> *But Jesus didn’t entrust himself to them, because he knew everyone,*
**25.** Akka namni tokko iyyuu waaʼee namaa isaaf dhugaa baʼu isa hin barbaachifne; inni waan nama keessa jiru beeka tureetii.
> *and because he didn’t need for anyone to testify concerning man; for he himself knew what was in man.*

## Boqonnaa 3 / Chapter 3

**1.** Fariisicha Niqoodemoos jedhamu bulchaa Yihuudootaa tokkotu ture.
> *Now there was a man of the Pharisees named Nicodemus, a ruler of the Jews.*
**2.** Innis halkaniin Yesuus bira dhaqee, “Yaa barsiisaa, akka ati barsiisaa Waaqa biraa dhufte taate nu beekna; nama Waaqni isa wajjin jiru malee namni mallattoo ati hojjettu kana hojjechuu dandaʼu tokko iyyuu hin jiruutii” jedheen.
> *He came to Jesus by night and said to him, “Rabbi, we know that you are a teacher who has come from God, for no one can do these signs that you do, unless God is with him.”*
**3.** Yesuusis deebisee, “Ani dhuguman, dhuguman sitti hima; namni yoo lammata dhalate malee mootummaa Waaqaa arguu hin dandaʼu” jedheen.
> *Jesus answered him, “Most certainly I tell you, unless one is born anew, he can’t see God’s Kingdom.”*
**4.** Niqoodemoos immoo, “Namni erga dulloomee booddee akkamitti dhalachuu dandaʼa? Dhalachuuf jedhee lammata gadameessa haadha isaa keessa seenuu dandaʼaa?” jedhee gaafate.
> *Nicodemus said to him, “How can a man be born when he is old? Can he enter a second time into his mother’s womb and be born?”*
**5.** Yesuusis akkana jedhee deebise; “Ani dhuguman, dhuguman sitti hima; namni yoo bishaanii fi Hafuura irraa dhalate malee mootummaa Waaqaatti galuu hin dandaʼu.
> *Jesus answered, “Most certainly I tell you, unless one is born of water and Spirit, he can’t enter into God’s Kingdom.*
**6.** Kan foon irraa dhalate foon; kan Hafuura irraa dhalate garuu hafuura.
> *That which is born of the flesh is flesh. That which is born of the Spirit is spirit.*
**7.** Jecha ani, ‘Ati lammata dhalachuu si barbaachisa siin jedhe hin dinqifatin.’
> *Don’t marvel that I said to you, ‘You must all be born anew.’*
**8.** Bubbeen gara fedhetti bubbisa. Atis huursuu isaa dhageessa malee akka inni eessaa dhufee fi akka inni garamitti deemu hin beektu; namni Hafuura irraa dhalate hundinuus akkanuma.”
> *The wind blows where it wants to, and you hear its sound, but don’t know where it comes from and where it is going. So is everyone who is born of the Spirit.”*
**9.** Niqoodemoosis, “Kun akkamitti taʼuu dandaʼa?” jedhee gaafate.
> *Nicodemus answered him, “How can these things be?”*
**10.** Yesuus akkana jedhee deebiseef; “Ati utuu barsiisaa Israaʼel taatee jirtuu waan kana hin beektuu?
> *Jesus answered him, “Are you the teacher of Israel, and don’t understand these things?*
**11.** Ani dhuguman, dhuguman sitti hima; nu waan beeknu dubbanna; waan argines dhugaa ni baana; isin garuu dhugaa baʼumsa keenya hin fudhattan.
> *Most certainly I tell you, we speak that which we know and testify of that which we have seen, and you don’t receive our witness.*
**12.** Isin yoo ani waan addunyaa isinitti himnaan hin amanin, yoo ani waan samii isinitti hime akkamitti amantu ree?
> *If I told you earthly things and you don’t believe, how will you believe if I tell you heavenly things?*
**13.** Ilma Namaa isa samii irraa gad buʼe malee eenyu iyyuu samiitti ol hin baane.
> *No one has ascended into heaven but he who descended out of heaven, the Son of Man, who is in heaven.*
**14.** Akkuma Museen gammoojjii keessatti bofa fannise sana Ilmi Namaas fannifamuu qaba.
> *As Moses lifted up the serpent in the wilderness, even so must the Son of Man be lifted up,*
**15.** Kunis akka namni isatti amanu hundi jireenya bara baraa qabaatuuf.”
> *that whoever believes in him should not perish, but have eternal life.*
**16.** Kan isatti amanu hundi jireenya bara baraa akka qabaatuuf malee akka hin badneef, Waaqni hamma Ilma isaa tokkicha kennutti addunyaa jaallateeraatii.
> *For God so loved the world, that he gave his only born Son, that whoever believes in him should not perish, but have eternal life.*
**17.** Waaqni karaa isaatiin addunyaa fayyisuudhaaf malee addunyaatti muruuf Ilma isaa gara addunyaatti hin ergineetii.
> *For God didn’t send his Son into the world to judge the world, but that the world should be saved through him.*
**18.** Kan isatti amanutti hin muramu; kan isatti hin amanne garuu waan inni maqaa Ilma Waaqaa tokkichaatti hin amaniniif ammuma iyyuu itti murameera.
> *He who believes in him is not judged. He who doesn’t believe has been judged already, because he has not believed in the name of the only born Son of God.*
**19.** Murtiin sunis isa kana: Ifni gara addunyaa dhufe; namoonni garuu waan hojiin isaanii hamaa tureef ifa caalaa dukkana jaallatan.
> *This is the judgment, that the light has come into the world, and men loved the darkness rather than the light, for their works were evil.*
**20.** Namni waan hamaa hojjetu kam iyyuu ifa jibba; akka hojiin isaa ifatti hin baafamneefis gara ifaa hin dhufu.
> *For everyone who does evil hates the light and doesn’t come to the light, lest his works would be exposed.*
**21.** Namni dhugaa hojjetu kam iyyuu garuu akka hojiin isaa akka fedhii Waaqaatti hojjetamuun isaa mulʼatuuf gara ifaa dhufa.
> *But he who does the truth comes to the light, that his works may be revealed, that they have been done in God.”*
**22.** Ergasiis Yesuusii fi barattoonni isaa gara biyya Yihuudaa dhaqan; innis isaan wajjin achi ture; ni cuuphes.
> *After these things, Jesus came with his disciples into the land of Judea. He stayed there with them and baptized.*
**23.** Yohannisis naannoo Saalem iddoo Enoon jedhamutti ni cuupha ture; bishaan baayʼeen achi tureetii; namoonnis achi dhaqanii cuuphamu turan.
> *John also was baptizing in Enon near Salim, because there was much water there. They came and were baptized;*
**24.** Kunis utuu Yohannis mana hidhaatti hin galfamin taʼe.
> *for John was not yet thrown into prison.*
**25.** Yommus barattoota Yohannisii fi Yihuudii tokko gidduutti waaʼee qulqulleeffamuu irratti wal falmiin uumame.
> *Therefore a dispute arose on the part of John’s disciples with some Jews about purification.*
**26.** Isaanis Yohannis bira dhufanii, “Yaa barsiisaa, namichi si wajjin Yordaanos gama ture, kan ati waaʼee isaa dhugaa baate sun kunoo cuuphuutti jira; namni hundis gara isaa dhaquutti jira” jedhaniin.
> *They came to John and said to him, “Rabbi, he who was with you beyond the Jordan, to whom you have testified, behold, he baptizes, and everyone is coming to him.”*
**27.** Yohannisis akkana jedhee deebise; “Namni yoo samii irraa isaaf kenname malee homaa fudhachuu hin dandaʼu.
> *John answered, “A man can receive nothing unless it has been given him from heaven.*
**28.** Akka ani, ‘Ani Kiristoos miti; garuu isa dura nan ergame’ jedhe isin mataan keessan iyyuu dhugaa naa baatu.
> *You yourselves testify that I said, ‘I am not the Christ,’ but, ‘I have been sent before him.’*
**29.** Misirrittiin kan misirrichaa ti. Miinjeen misirricha bira dhaabatuu fi isa dhaggeeffatu, yommuu sagalee misirrichaa dhagaʼutti akka malee gammada. Gammachuun sun kan koo ti; gammachuun koos amma guutuu taʼeera.
> *He who has the bride is the bridegroom; but the friend of the bridegroom, who stands and hears him, rejoices greatly because of the bridegroom’s voice. Therefore my joy is made full.*
**30.** Inni guddaa taʼuu qaba; ani immoo xinnaa taʼuun qaba.
> *He must increase, but I must decrease.*
**31.** “Kan olii dhufu inni hundumaa oli; inni lafaa immoo kanuma lafaa ti; waanuma lafaa dubbatas. Kan samii irraa dhufu inni hundumaa oli.
> *“He who comes from above is above all. He who is from the earth belongs to the earth and speaks of the earth. He who comes from heaven is above all.*
**32.** Inni waan argee fi waan dhagaʼe dhugaa baʼa; dhuga baʼumsa isaa garuu eenyu iyyuu hin fudhatu.
> *What he has seen and heard, of that he testifies; and no one receives his witness.*
**33.** Namni dhuga baʼumsa kana fudhates akka Waaqni dhugaa taʼe mirkaneesse.
> *He who has received his witness has set his seal to this, that God is true.*
**34.** Inni Waaqni erge dubbii Waaqaa dubbata; Waaqnis safara malee Hafuura isaa ni kennaatii.
> *For he whom God has sent speaks the words of God; for God gives the Spirit without measure.*
**35.** Abbaan Ilma ni jaallata; waan hundas harka isaatti kenneera.
> *The Father loves the Son, and has given all things into his hand.*
**36.** Namni Ilmatti amanu jireenya bara baraa qaba; namni Ilmaaf hin ajajamne garuu dheekkamsa Waaqaatu isa irra jiraata malee jireenya hin argu.”
> *One who believes in the Son has eternal life, but one who disobeys the Son won’t see life, but the wrath of God remains on him.”*

## Boqonnaa 4 / Chapter 4

**1.** Egaa Yesuus Yohannis caalaa barattoota akka baayʼifatee fi akka cuuphe Fariisonni dhagaʼuu isaanii Gooftaan ni beeke;
> *Therefore when the Lord knew that the Pharisees had heard that Jesus was making and baptizing more disciples than John*
**2.** taʼus barattoota isaatu cuuphaa ture malee Yesuus ofii isaatii hin cuuphine.
> *(although Jesus himself didn’t baptize, but his disciples),*
**3.** Inni Yihuudaa dhiisee amma illee Galiilaatti deebiʼe.
> *he left Judea and departed into Galilee.*
**4.** Innis Samaariyaa keessa darbuu qaba ture.
> *He needed to pass through Samaria.*
**5.** Kanaafuu gara magaalaa Samaariyaa kan Siikaar jedhamu tokkoo dhufe; magaalaan sunis lafa Yaaqoob ilma isaa Yoosefiif kenne sanatti dhiʼoo dha.
> *So he came to a city of Samaria called Sychar, near the parcel of ground that Jacob gave to his son Joseph.*
**6.** Boolli bishaanii kan Yaaqoob tokko achi ture; Yesuusis waan karaa deemuudhaan dadhabeef boolla bishaanii sana bira taaʼe. Yeroonis gara saʼaatii jaʼaa ture.
> *Jacob’s well was there. Jesus therefore, being tired from his journey, sat down by the well. It was about the sixth hour.*
**7.** Dubartiin Samaariyaa tokkos bishaan waraabbachuu dhufte; Yesuusis, “Mee na obaasi” jedheen.
> *A woman of Samaria came to draw water. Jesus said to her, “Give me a drink.”*
**8.** Barattoonni isaa nyaata bituuf gara magaalaa dhaqanii turaniitii.
> *For his disciples had gone away into the city to buy food.*
**9.** Dubartiin Samaariyaa sunis Yesuusiin, “Ati utuu Yihuudii taatee jirtuu akkamitti ‘na obaasi’ jettee ana dubartii Samaariyaa kadhatta?” jetteen. Yihuudoonni warra Samaariyaa wajjin walitti dhufeenya hin qaban tureetii.
> *The Samaritan woman therefore said to him, “How is it that you, being a Jew, ask for a drink from me, a Samaritan woman?” (For Jews have no dealings with Samaritans.)*
**10.** Yesuusis deebisee, “Kennaa Waaqaatii fi inni, ‘na obaasi’ siin jedhu kun eenyu akka taʼe utuu beektee silaa situ isa kadhata ture; innis bishaan jireenyaa siif kenna ture” jedheen.
> *Jesus answered her, “If you knew the gift of God, and who it is who says to you, ‘Give me a drink,’ you would have asked him, and he would have given you living water.”*
**11.** Dubartittiinis akkana jetteen; “Yaa gooftaa, ati waan ittiin waraabbattu hin qabdu; boolli bishaanii kunis gad fagoo dha. Yoos bishaan jireenyaa eessaa argatta?
> *The woman said to him, “Sir, you have nothing to draw with, and the well is deep. So where do you get that living water?*
**12.** Ati abbaa keenya Yaaqoob isa boolla bishaanii kana nuu kenne caalta moo? Inni mataan isaa, ijoolleen isaatii fi horiin isaa boolla bishaanii kana keessaa dhugaa turan.”
> *Are you greater than our father Jacob, who gave us the well and drank from it himself, as did his children and his livestock?”*
**13.** Yesuus immoo akkana jedhee deebise; “Namni bishaan kana dhugu hundinuu deebiʼee ni dheebota;
> *Jesus answered her, “Everyone who drinks of this water will thirst again,*
**14.** garuu namni bishaan ani isaaf kennu dhugu kam iyyuu gonkumaa hin dheebotu. Bishaan ani isaaf kennu isa keessatti burqaa bishaanii isa jireenya bara baraatiif burqu ni taʼa.”
> *but whoever drinks of the water that I will give him will never thirst again; but the water that I will give him will become in him a well of water springing up to eternal life.”*
**15.** Dubartittiinis, “Yaa Gooftaa, akka ani hin dheebonneef, bishaan waraabuufis akka ani as hin dhufneef bishaan sana naa kenni” jetteen.
> *The woman said to him, “Sir, give me this water, so that I don’t get thirsty, neither come all the way here to draw.”*
**16.** Yesuus immoo, “Dhaqiitii dhirsa kee waamii kottu!” jedheen.
> *Jesus said to her, “Go, call your husband, and come here.”*
**17.** Dubartittiin, “Ani dhirsa hin qabu” jetteen. Yesuusis akkana jedheen; “Dhirsa hin qabu jechuun kee sirrii dha;
> *The woman answered, “I have no husband.” Jesus said to her, “You said well, ‘I have no husband,’*
**18.** ati dhirsoota shan qabda turteetii; namichi amma si wajjin jirus dhirsa kee miti. Wanni ati amma dubbate dhugaa dha.”
> *for you have had five husbands; and he whom you now have is not your husband. This you have said truly.”*
**19.** Dubartittiinis akkana jette; “Yaa Gooftaa, akka ati raajii taate nan hubadha.
> *The woman said to him, “Sir, I perceive that you are a prophet.*
**20.** Abbootiin keenya tulluu kana irratti sagadaa turan; isin Yihuudoonni garuu iddoon namni itti waaqeffachuu qabu Yerusaalem jettu.”
> *Our fathers worshiped in this mountain, and you Jews say that in Jerusalem is the place where people ought to worship.”*
**21.** Yesuusis akkana jedheen; “Dubartii nana, na amani; yeroon isin itti tulluu kana irrattis taʼu yookaan Yerusaalemitti Abbaa hin waaqeffanne tokko ni dhufa.
> *Jesus said to her, “Woman, believe me, the hour is coming when neither in this mountain nor in Jerusalem will you worship the Father.*
**22.** Isin waan hin beekne waaqeffattu; nu sababii fayyinni Yihuudoota keessaa dhufuuf kan beeknu waaqeffanna.
> *You worship that which you don’t know. We worship that which we know; for salvation is from the Jews.*
**23.** Warri dhugaan waaqeffatan yeroon isaan itti hafuuraa fi dhugaadhaan Abbaa waaqeffatan tokko ni dhufa; amma iyyuu dhufeera; Abbaan warra akkasitti isa waaqeffatan barbaada.
> *But the hour comes, and now is, when the true worshipers will worship the Father in spirit and truth, for the Father seeks such to be his worshipers.*
**24.** Waaqni hafuura; warri isa waaqeffatanis Hafuuraa fi dhugaadhaan waaqeffachuu qabu.”
> *God is spirit, and those who worship him must worship in spirit and truth.”*
**25.** Dubartittiinis, “Ani akka Masiihiin Kiristoos jedhamu dhufu nan beeka. Inni yommuu dhufutti waan hunda nutti hima” jetteen.
> *The woman said to him, “I know that Messiah is coming, he who is called Christ. When he has come, he will declare to us all things.”*
**26.** Yesuusis, “Ani, namni amma sitti dubbachaa jiru kun isuma” jedheen.
> *Jesus said to her, “I am he, the one who speaks to you.”*
**27.** Barattoonni isaa yommusuma deebiʼanii utuu inni dubartii tokko wajjin dubbatuu arganii dinqisiifatan. Garuu namni, “Maal barbaadda?” yookaan “Maaliif ishee wajjin haasofta?” jedhee isa gaafatu tokko iyyuu hin turre.
> *Just then, his disciples came. They marveled that he was speaking with a woman; yet no one said, “What are you looking for?” or, “Why do you speak with her?”*
**28.** Dubartiin sunis okkotee ishee dhiiftee gara magaalaatti deebite; namootaanis akkana jette;
> *So the woman left her water pot, went away into the city, and said to the people,*
**29.** “Kottaatii nama waan ani hojjedhe hunda natti hime tokko ilaalaa! Tarii inni Kiristoos taʼinnaa?”
> *“Come, see a man who told me everything that I have done. Can this be the Christ?”*
**30.** Isaanis magaalaa keessaa baʼanii Yesuus bira dhufan.
> *They went out of the city, and were coming to him.*
**31.** Kana gidduutti barattoonni isaa, “Yaa barsiisaa, mee waa nyaadhu!” jedhanii isa kadhatan.
> *In the meanwhile, the disciples urged him, saying, “Rabbi, eat.”*
**32.** Inni garuu, “Ani nyaata isin hin beekne kanan nyaadhu qaba” jedheen.
> *But he said to them, “I have food to eat that you don’t know about.”*
**33.** Barattoonni isaas, “Namatu nyaata fideefii laata?” jedhanii wal gaafatan.
> *The disciples therefore said to one another, “Has anyone brought him something to eat?”*
**34.** Yesuus immoo akkana jedheen; “Nyaanni koo fedhii isa na ergee guutuu fi hojii isaa raawwachuu dha.
> *Jesus said to them, “My food is to do the will of him who sent me and to accomplish his work.*
**35.** Isin, ‘Jiʼa afurtu hafe; ergasii midhaan walitti qabama’ jettu mitii? Kunoo, ani isinittan hima; akka midhaan bilchaatee walitti qabamuu gaʼe mee ol jedhaatii lafa qotiisaa ilaalaa!
> *Don’t you say, ‘There are yet four months until the harvest’? Behold, I tell you, lift up your eyes and look at the fields, that they are white for harvest already.*
**36.** Inni midhaan haamu amma iyyuu mindaa ni argata; jireenya bara baraatiif ija walitti ni qaba; kunis akka inni facaasuu fi inni walitti qabu waliin gammadaniif.
> *He who reaps receives wages and gathers fruit to eternal life, that both he who sows and he who reaps may rejoice together.*
**37.** Haala kanaan mammaaksi, ‘Inni tokko ni facaasa; kaan immoo ni haama’ jedhu sun dhugaa dha.
> *For in this the saying is true, ‘One sows, and another reaps.’*
**38.** Ani akka isin waan itti hin dadhabin haammattaniifan isin erge. Warri kaan itti dadhabaniiru; isin garuu buʼaa dadhabbii isaanii galfattu.”
> *I sent you to reap that for which you haven’t labored. Others have labored, and you have entered into their labor.”*
**39.** Sababii dubartittiin, “Inni waan ani hojjedhe hunda natti hime” jettee dhugaa baateef namoonni Samaariyaa warri magaalaa sanaa baayʼeen isatti amanan.
> *From that city many of the Samaritans believed in him because of the word of the woman, who testified, “He told me everything that I have done.”*
**40.** Kanaafuu namoonni Samaariyaa yommuu gara isaa dhufanitti akka inni isaan wajjin turuuf isa kadhatan; innis guyyaa lama achi ture.
> *So when the Samaritans came to him, they begged him to stay with them. He stayed there two days.*
**41.** Sababii dubbii isaatiinis namoonni biraa baayʼeen isatti amanan.
> *Many more believed because of his word.*
**42.** Isaanis dubartittiidhaan, “Siʼachi wanni nu amannuuf sababii dubbii ati nutti himte sanaatiif miti; nu mataan keenya iyyuu isa dhageenyeerraatii; akka inni dhugumaan Fayyisaa addunyaa taʼes beekneerra” jedhan.
> *They said to the woman, “Now we believe, not because of your speaking; for we have heard for ourselves, and know that this is indeed the Christ, the Savior of the world.”*
**43.** Guyyaa lamaan sana booddee Yesuus Galiilaa dhaqe.
> *After the two days he went out from there and went into Galilee.*
**44.** Yesuus mataan isaa iyyuu akka raajiin tokko biyya isaa keessatti kabaja hin qabne dubbatee tureetii.
> *For Jesus himself testified that a prophet has no honor in his own country.*
**45.** Yommuu inni Galiilaa gaʼettis warri Galiilaa isa simatan. Isaanis sababii Yerusaalem turaniif waan inni yeroo Ayyaana Faasiikaatti achitti hojjete hunda arganii turan.
> *So when he came into Galilee, the Galileans received him, having seen all the things that he did in Jerusalem at the feast, for they also went to the feast.*
**46.** Kanaafuu inni deebiʼee gara Qaanaa ishee Galiilaa keessaa iddoo itti bishaan gara daadhii wayiniitti geeddaree ture sanaa dhaqe. Qondaalli mootii kan ilmi isaa dhukkubsate tokkos Qifirnaahom keessa ture.
> *Jesus came therefore again to Cana of Galilee, where he made the water into wine. There was a certain nobleman whose son was sick at Capernaum.*
**47.** Namichi kunis yommuu akka Yesuus Yihuudaadhaa kaʼee Galiilaa dhufe dhagaʼetti isa bira dhaqee akka inni dhufee ilma isaa kan duʼuu gaʼe sana fayyisu isa kadhate.
> *When he heard that Jesus had come out of Judea into Galilee, he went to him and begged him that he would come down and heal his son, for he was at the point of death.*
**48.** Kana irratti Yesuus, “Isin yoo mallattoo fi dinqii argitan malee hin amantan!” isaan jedhe.
> *Jesus therefore said to him, “Unless you people see signs and wonders, you will in no way believe.”*
**49.** Qondaaltichis, “Yaa Gooftaa, maaloo utuu mucaan koo hin duʼin naa qaqqabi” jedheen.
> *The nobleman said to him, “Sir, come down before my child dies.”*
**50.** Yesuusis, “Ilmi kee ni jiraataatii gali!” jedheen. Namichis waan Yesuus isatti dubbate amanee deeme.
> *Jesus said to him, “Go your way. Your son lives.” The man believed the word that Jesus spoke to him, and he went his way.*
**51.** Utuu inni deemaa jiruus tajaajiltoonni isaa itti dhufanii akka ilmi isaa lubbuun jiru itti himan.
> *As he was going down, his servants met him and reported, saying “Your child lives!”*
**52.** Innis yeroo itti mucaa isaatti wayyaaʼe gaafate; isaanis, “Kaleessa saʼaatii torbatti dhagna gubaan isa dhiise” jedhaniin.
> *So he inquired of them the hour when he began to get better. They said therefore to him, “Yesterday at the seventh hour, the fever left him.”*
**53.** Abbaan mucichaas akka yeroon sun yeroo Yesuus itti, “Ilmi kee ni jiraata” jedheen taʼe hubate. Kanaafuu innis, warri mana isaa hundinuus ni amanan.
> *So the father knew that it was at that hour in which Jesus said to him, “Your son lives.” He believed, as did his whole house.*
**54.** Kun mallattoo lammaffaa Yesuus erga Yihuudaadhaa Galiilaa dhufee hojjetee dha.
> *This is again the second sign that Jesus did, having come out of Judea into Galilee.*

## Boqonnaa 5 / Chapter 5

**1.** Yeroo xinnoo booddee ayyaana Yihuudootaa tokkotu ture; Yesuusis Yerusaalemitti ol baʼe.
> *After these things, there was a feast of the Jews, and Jesus went up to Jerusalem.*
**2.** Yerusaalem keessa Karra Hoolaa bira haroo afaan Ibraayisxiitiin Beetizaataa jedhamu tokkotu jira; haroon sun gardaafoo shan qaba.
> *Now in Jerusalem by the sheep gate, there is a pool, which is called in Hebrew, “Bethesda”, having five porches.*
**3.** Gardaafoowwan sana keessas namoonni dhukkubsatan, jechuunis jaamonni, warri naafatanii fi warri dhagni irratti duʼe baayʼeen ciisanii raafama bishaanii eeggachaa turan. [
> *In these lay a great multitude of those who were sick, blind, lame, or paralyzed, waiting for the moving of the water;*
**4.** Ergamaan Gooftaa yeroo yerootti gad buʼee bishaanicha ni raasa ture; raafama bishaanichaa booddee namni jalqabatti bishaan sana keessa seenu kam iyyuu dhukkuba qabu hunda irraa ni fayya ture.]
> *for an angel went down at certain times into the pool and stirred up the water. Whoever stepped in first after the stirring of the water was healed of whatever disease he had.*
**5.** Namichi waggaa soddomii saddeeti dhukkubsate tokko achi ture.
> *A certain man was there who had been sick for thirty-eight years.*
**6.** Yesuusis yommuu isaa achi ciisu argee akka inni yeroo dheeraa haala kana keessa ture beeketti, “Ati fayyuu barbaaddaa?” jedhee isa gaafate.
> *When Jesus saw him lying there, and knew that he had been sick for a long time, he asked him, “Do you want to be made well?”*
**7.** Dhukkubsataan sunis, “Yaa Gooftaa, ani nama na gargaaree yommuu bishaanichi raafamutti haroo sana keessa na buusu hin qabu. Yeroo ani seenuu yaaluttis namni biraa na dursee seena” jedheen.
> *The sick man answered him, “Sir, I have no one to put me into the pool when the water is stirred up, but while I’m coming, another steps down before me.”*
**8.** Kana irratti Yesuus, “Kaʼi! Siree kee fudhadhuutii deemi” jedheen.
> *Jesus said to him, “Arise, take up your mat, and walk.”*
**9.** Namichis yeruma sana fayye; siree isaas fudhatee deeme. Guyyaan wanni kun taʼe sunis Sanbata ture;
> *Immediately, the man was made well, and took up his mat and walked. Now that day was a Sabbath.*
**10.** Yihuudoonnis namicha fayye sanaan, “Harʼa Sanbata; seerri siree kee baachuu si dhowwa” jedhaniin.
> *So the Jews said to him who was cured, “It is the Sabbath. It is not lawful for you to carry the mat.”*
**11.** Inni garuu, “Namicha na fayyisetu ‘Siree kee fudhadhuu deemi’ naan jedhe” jedheen.
> *He answered them, “He who made me well said to me, ‘Take up your mat and walk.’ ”*
**12.** Isaanis, “Namichi siree kee fudhadhuu deemi siin jedhe sun eenyu?” jedhanii isa gaafatan.
> *Then they asked him, “Who is the man who said to you, ‘Take up your mat and walk’?”*
**13.** Namichi fayye sun sababii Yesuus achi hiiqee tuuta iddoo sana ture keessa seeneef inni eenyu akka taʼe hin beekne.
> *But he who was healed didn’t know who it was, for Jesus had withdrawn, a crowd being in the place.*
**14.** Yesuusis ergasii namicha sana mana qulqullummaa keessatti argee, “Ati kunoo amma fayyiteerta. Wanni kana caalu akka sitti hin dhufneef siʼachi cubbuu hin hojjetin” jedheen.
> *Afterward Jesus found him in the temple and said to him, “Behold, you are made well. Sin no more, so that nothing worse happens to you.”*
**15.** Namichis dhaqee kan isa fayyise Yesuus akka taʼe Yihuudootatti hime.
> *The man went away, and told the Jews that it was Jesus who had made him well.*
**16.** Kanaafuu sababii inni Sanbataan waan kana hojjeteef, Yihuudoonni Yesuusin ariʼachuu jalqaban.
> *For this cause the Jews persecuted Jesus and sought to kill him, because he did these things on the Sabbath.*
**17.** Yesuus garuu, “Abbaan koo hamma ammaatti hojjechaa jira; anis hojjechaan jira” isaaniin jedhe.
> *But Jesus answered them, “My Father is still working, so I am working, too.”*
**18.** Sababii kanaaf Yihuudoonni ittuma caalchisanii isa ajjeesuu barbaadan; inni Waaqaan of qixxeessuudhaan Waaqa iyyuu Abbaa ofii isaa godhee waamaa ture malee Sanbata qofa hin cabsine.
> *For this cause therefore the Jews sought all the more to kill him, because he not only broke the Sabbath, but also called God his own Father, making himself equal with God.*
**19.** Yesuus immoo akkana jedhee deebiseef; “Ani dhuguman, dhuguman isinitti hima; Ilmi ofii isaatiin waan tokko illee hojjechuu hin dandaʼu; inni waanuma utuu Abbaan isaa hojjetuu arge qofa hojjechuu dandaʼa; waanuma Abbaan hojjetu Ilmis akkasuma hojjetaatii.
> *Jesus therefore answered them, “Most certainly, I tell you, the Son can do nothing of himself, but what he sees the Father doing. For whatever things he does, these the Son also does likewise.*
**20.** Abbaan Ilma ni jaallataatii; waan hojjetu hundumas isatti ni argisiisa. Akka isin dinqisiifattaniifis inni hojiiwwan hojiiwwan kanneen caalan iyyuu isatti ni argisiisa.
> *For the Father has affection for the Son, and shows him all things that he himself does. He will show him greater works than these, that you may marvel.*
**21.** Akkuma Abbaan warra duʼan duʼaa kaasee jireenya kennuuf sana, Ilmis akkasuma abbaa fedheef jireenya ni kenna.
> *For as the Father raises the dead and gives them life, even so the Son also gives life to whom he desires.*
**22.** Abbaan eenyutti illee hin muru; murtii hunda garuu Ilmatti kenneera;
> *For the Father judges no one, but he has given all judgment to the Son,*
**23.** kunis akka namni hundi akkuma Abbaaf ulfina kennutti Ilmaafis ulfina kennuuf. Namni Ilmaaf ulfina hin kennine, Abbaa isa Ilma ergeefis ulfina hin kennu.
> *that all may honor the Son, even as they honor the Father. He who doesn’t honor the Son doesn’t honor the Father who sent him.*
**24.** “Ani dhuguman, dhuguman isinitti hima; namni dubbii koo dhagaʼu, isa na ergettis kan amanu hundi jireenya bara baraa qaba; murtiittis hin dhiʼaatu; inni duʼa jalaa gara jireenyaatti darbeera.
> *“Most certainly I tell you, he who hears my word and believes him who sent me has eternal life, and doesn’t come into judgment, but has passed out of death into life.*
**25.** Ani dhuguman, dhuguman isinitti hima; yeroon warri duʼan itti sagalee Ilma Waaqaa dhagaʼan tokko ni dhufa; ammuma iyyuu dhufeera; warri dhagaʼanis ni jiraatu.
> *Most certainly I tell you, the hour comes, and now is, when the dead will hear the Son of God’s voice; and those who hear will live.*
**26.** Akkuma Abbaan ofuma isaatiin jireenya qabu sana, Ilmis akkasuma akka ofuma isaatiin jireenya qabaatuuf isaaf kenneeraatii.
> *For as the Father has life in himself, even so he gave to the Son also to have life in himself.*
**27.** Sababii inni Ilma Namaa taʼeefis Abbaan akka inni murtii kennuuf taayitaa isaaf kenneera.
> *He also gave him authority to execute judgment, because he is a son of man.*
**28.** “Kana hin dinqisiifatinaa; saʼaatiin itti warri awwaala keessa jiran hundi sagalee isaa dhagaʼan tokko ni dhufaatii.
> *Don’t marvel at this, for the hour comes in which all who are in the tombs will hear his voice*
**29.** Warri waan gaarii hojjetan duʼaa kaʼuu jireenyaatiif, warri waan hamaa hojjetan immoo duʼaa kaʼuu murtiitiif duʼaa ni kaʼu.
> *and will come out; those who have done good, to the resurrection of life; and those who have done evil, to the resurrection of judgment.*
**30.** Ani ofii kootiin waan tokko illee gochuu hin dandaʼu; ani akkuman dhagaʼutti nan murteessa; ani fedhii isa na ergee malee fedhii mataa kootii waan hin barbaanneef murtiin koo qajeelaa dha.
> *I can of myself do nothing. As I hear, I judge; and my judgment is righteous, because I don’t seek my own will, but the will of my Father who sent me.*
**31.** “Ani yoon waaʼee ofii kootii dhugaa baʼe, dhuga baʼumsi koo dhugaa miti.
> *“If I testify about myself, my witness is not valid.*
**32.** Garuu kan waaʼee koo dhugaa baʼu biraa jira; anis akka dhuga baʼumsi inni waaʼee koo kennu sun dhugaa taʼe beeka.
> *It is another who testifies about me. I know that the testimony which he testifies about me is true.*
**33.** “Isin Yohannisitti ergitaniirtu; innis dhugaa sanaaf, dhugaa baʼeera.
> *You have sent to John, and he has testified to the truth.*
**34.** Ani dhuga baʼumsa namaa hin fudhadhu; garuu akka isin fayyitaniif wantoota kanneen nan dubbadha.
> *But the testimony which I receive is not from man. However, I say these things that you may be saved.*
**35.** Yohannis ibsaa bobaʼee ifa kennu ture; isinis yeroo xinnoo ifa isaatti gammaduu barbaaddan.
> *He was the burning and shining lamp, and you were willing to rejoice for a while in his light.*
**36.** “Ani garuu dhuga baʼumsa, dhuga baʼumsa Yohannis sana caalu qaba. Hojiin akka ani raawwadhuuf Abbaan natti kenne, kan ani hojjedhu kun iyyuu akka Abbaan na erge mirkaneessaatii.
> *But the testimony which I have is greater than that of John; for the works which the Father gave me to accomplish, the very works that I do, testify about me, that the Father has sent me.*
**37.** Abbaan inni na erges ofii isaatii waaʼee koo dhugaa baʼeera. Isin takkumaa sagalee isaa hin dhageenye; bifa isaas takkumaa hin argine.
> *The Father himself, who sent me, has testified about me. You have neither heard his voice at any time, nor seen his form.*
**38.** Isin sababii isa inni erge hin amaniniif dubbiin isaa isin keessa hin jiraatu.
> *You don’t have his word living in you, because you don’t believe him whom he sent.*
**39.** Isin Katabbiiwwan Qulqulluudhaan jireenya bara baraa arganna jettanii waan yaaddaniif Katabbiiwwan Qulqulluu sana qorattu. Katabbiiwwan Qulqulluun kunneenis waaʼee koo dhugaa baʼu;
> *“You search the Scriptures, because you think that in them you have eternal life; and these are they which testify about me.*
**40.** isin garuu jireenya argachuudhaaf gara koo dhufuu hin barbaaddan.
> *Yet you will not come to me, that you may have life.*
**41.** “Ani nama irraa ulfina hin fudhadhu;
> *I don’t receive glory from men.*
**42.** garuu akka isin jaalala Waaqaa of keessaa hin qabne nan beeka.
> *But I know you, that you don’t have God’s love in yourselves.*
**43.** Ani maqaa Abbaa kootiin dhufeera; isin na hin simattan; utuu namni biraa maqaa ofii isaatiin dhufee garuu isa ni simattu.
> *I have come in my Father’s name, and you don’t receive me. If another comes in his own name, you will receive him.*
**44.** Isin yoo wal irraa ulfina argattanii ulfina Waaqa tokkicha biraa dhufu garuu hin barbaanne akkamitti amanuu dandeessu ree?
> *How can you believe, who receive glory from one another, and you don’t seek the glory that comes from the only God?*
**45.** “Waan ani Abbaa duratti isin himadhu hin seʼinaa. Kan isin himatu tokko jira; innis Musee isin abdattan sana.
> *“Don’t think that I will accuse you to the Father. There is one who accuses you, even Moses, on whom you have set your hope.*
**46.** Utuu Musee amantanii silaa anas ni amantu ture; inni waaʼee koo barreesseetii.
> *For if you believed Moses, you would believe me; for he wrote about me.*
**47.** Isin erga waan inni barreesse hin amannee, dubbii koo immoo akkamitti amantu ree?”
> *But if you don’t believe his writings, how will you believe my words?”*

## Boqonnaa 6 / Chapter 6

**1.** Wantoota kanneen booddee Yesuus Galaana Galiilaa isa Galaana Xibeeriyaas jedhamu gama ceʼe;
> *After these things, Jesus went away to the other side of the sea of Galilee, which is also called the Sea of Tiberias.*
**2.** namoonni baayʼeenis sababii mallattoo inni warra dhukkubsatan fayyisuudhaan hojjete arganiif isa duukaa buʼan.
> *A great multitude followed him, because they saw his signs which he did on those who were sick.*
**3.** Yesuusis barattoota isaa wajjin gaaratti ol baʼee taaʼe.
> *Jesus went up into the mountain, and he sat there with his disciples.*
**4.** Yeroo sana Ayyaanni Yihuudootaa, Faasiikaan dhiʼaatee ture.
> *Now the Passover, the feast of the Jews, was at hand.*
**5.** Yesuus yommuu ol ilaaletti tuuta guddaa gara isaa dhufaa jiru tokko arge; Fiiliphoosiinis, “Namoota kanneeniif buddeena isaan nyaatan eessaa bitanna?” jedhe.
> *Jesus therefore, lifting up his eyes and seeing that a great multitude was coming to him, said to Philip, “Where are we to buy bread, that these may eat?”*
**6.** Kanas Fiiliphoosin qoruudhaaf jedhee gaafate; inni mataan isaa waan gochuu qabu beeka tureetii.
> *He said this to test him, for he himself knew what he would do.*
**7.** Fiiliphoos immoo, “Akka tokkoon tokkoon isaanii waanuma xinnoo illee argataniif buddeena gaʼu bituuf diinaarii dhibba lama fudhata” jedhee deebiseef.
> *Philip answered him, “Two hundred denarii worth of bread is not sufficient for them, that every one of them may receive a little.”*
**8.** Barattoota isaa keessaa tokko Indiriiyaas, obboleessi Simoon Phexros akkana jedheen;
> *One of his disciples, Andrew, Simon Peter’s brother, said to him,*
**9.** “Gurbaan buddeena garbuu shanii fi qurxummii lama qabu tokko kunoo ti; garuu nama akkana baayʼatu kanaaf kun maali?”
> *“There is a boy here who has five barley loaves and two fish, but what are these among so many?”*
**10.** Yesuusis, “Namoota teessisaa” jedhe. Iddoo sanas marga baayʼeetu ture; namoonnis ni tataaʼan; dhiira gara kuma shantu achi ture.
> *Jesus said, “Have the people sit down.” Now there was much grass in that place. So the men sat down, in number about five thousand.*
**11.** Yesuusis ergasii buddeena fuudhee, galata galchee warra tataaʼaniif hamma isaan barbaadan qoqqoode. Qurxummii sana illee akkasuma godhe.
> *Jesus took the loaves, and having given thanks, he distributed to the disciples, and the disciples to those who were sitting down, likewise also of the fish as much as they desired.*
**12.** Innis yommuu isaan hundi nyaatanii quufanitti barattoota isaatiin, “Hurraaʼaa hambifame walitti qabaa. Wanni tokko iyyuu hin badin” jedhe.
> *When they were filled, he said to his disciples, “Gather up the broken pieces which are left over, that nothing be lost.”*
**13.** Kanaafuu isaan hurraaʼaa buddeena garbuu shananii kan namoonni nyaatanii hambisan walitti qabanii gundoo kudha lama guutan.
> *So they gathered them up, and filled twelve baskets with broken pieces from the five barley loaves, which were left over by those who had eaten.*
**14.** Namoonnis yommuu mallattoo Yesuus hojjete arganitti, “Kun dhugumaanuu Raajii gara addunyaa dhufuuf jiru” jedhan.
> *When therefore the people saw the sign which Jesus did, they said, “This is truly the prophet who comes into the world.”*
**15.** Yesuusis akka namoonni sun dhufanii humnaan qabanii mootii isa gochuu yaadan hubatee ammas kophaa isaa gara tulluu deeme.
> *Jesus therefore, perceiving that they were about to come and take him by force to make him king, withdrew again to the mountain by himself.*
**16.** Yommuu galgalaaʼetti barattoonni isaa gara galaanaatti gad buʼan;
> *When evening came, his disciples went down to the sea.*
**17.** achittis bidiruu yaabbatanii galaana irra gama Qifirnaahomitti qajeelan. Yeroon sun dukkana ture; Yesuus amma iyyuu gara isaanii hin dhufne ture.
> *They entered into the boat, and were going over the sea to Capernaum. It was now dark, and Jesus had not come to them.*
**18.** Bubbeen jabaan isaa waan bubbisaa tureef bishaanichi ni raafame.
> *The sea was tossed by a great wind blowing.*
**19.** Isaanis erga gara kiiloo meetira shanii yookaan jaʼaa mooqanii booddee utuu Yesuus galaana irra deemaa, bidiruu sanattis dhiʼaachaa jiruu argan; ni sodaatanis.
> *When therefore they had rowed about twenty-five or thirty stadia, they saw Jesus walking on the sea and drawing near to the boat; and they were afraid.*
**20.** Inni garuu, “Anumaatii hin sodaatinaa” jedheen.
> *But he said to them, “It is I. Don’t be afraid.”*
**21.** Isaanis bidiruutti ol isa fudhachuu barbaadan; bidiruun sunis yommusuma lafa isaan dhaqaa turan sana gaʼe.
> *They were willing therefore to receive him into the boat. Immediately the boat was at the land where they were going.*
**22.** Guyyaa itti aanutti namoonni galaanicha gamatti hafanii turan akka bidiruun tokko qofti achi ture, akka Yesuus barattoota isaa wajjin bidiruu hin yaabbatinii fi akka isaan kophaa isaanii deeman hubatan.
> *On the next day, the multitude that stood on the other side of the sea saw that there was no other boat there, except the one in which his disciples had embarked, and that Jesus hadn’t entered with his disciples into the boat, but his disciples had gone away alone.*
**23.** Bidiruuwwan biraa Xibeeriyaasii kaʼanii iddoo itti Gooftaan galata galchee isaanis itti buddeena nyaatan sana bira gaʼan.
> *However, boats from Tiberias came near to the place where they ate the bread after the Lord had given thanks.*
**24.** Namoonni sunis yommuu akka Yesuusii fi barattoonni isaa achi hin jirre baranitti bidiruuwwan yaabbatanii Yesuusin barbaaduuf Qifirnaahom dhaqan.
> *When the multitude therefore saw that Jesus wasn’t there, nor his disciples, they themselves got into the boats and came to Capernaum, seeking Jesus.*
**25.** Namoonni yommuu galaanicha gamatti isa arganitti, “Yaa Barsiisaa, ati yoom as dhufte?” jedhanii isa gaafatan.
> *When they found him on the other side of the sea, they asked him, “Rabbi, when did you come here?”*
**26.** Yesuusis akkana jedhee deebise; “Ani dhuguman, dhuguman isinitti hima; isin waan mallattoo argitaniif utuu hin taʼin, waan buddeena nyaattanii quuftaniif na barbaaddu.
> *Jesus answered them, “Most certainly I tell you, you seek me, not because you saw signs, but because you ate of the loaves and were filled.*
**27.** Nyaata jireenya bara baraatiif taʼu kan Ilmi Namaa isinii kennuuf dadhabaa malee nyaata baduuf hin dadhabinaa. Waaqni Abbaan chaappaa isaa kan mirkaneessu Ilma irratti rukuteeraatii.”
> *Don’t work for the food which perishes, but for the food which remains to eternal life, which the Son of Man will give to you. For God the Father has sealed him.”*
**28.** Isaanis, “Nu akka hojii Waaqaa hojjennuuf maal gochuu qabna?” jedhanii isa gaafatan.
> *They said therefore to him, “What must we do, that we may work the works of God?”*
**29.** Yesuusis, “Isa inni ergetti amanuun keessan kun hojii Waaqaa ti” jedhee deebiseef.
> *Jesus answered them, “This is the work of God, that you believe in him whom he has sent.”*
**30.** Kanaafuu isaan akkana jedhanii isa gaafatan; “Akka nu isa arginee si amannuuf, mallattoo maalii argisiifta? Maal hojjettas?
> *They said therefore to him, “What then do you do for a sign, that we may see and believe you? What work do you do?*
**31.** ‘Akka isaan nyaataniif samii irraa buddeena isaaniif kenne’ jedhamee akkuma barreeffame, abbootiin keenya gammoojjii keessatti mannaa nyaatan.”
> *Our fathers ate the manna in the wilderness. As it is written, ‘He gave them bread out of heaven to eat.’”*
**32.** Yesuusis akkana jedheen; “Ani dhuguman isinitti hima; kan samii irraa buddeena isinii kenne Musee miti; kan samii irraa buddeena dhugaa isiniif kennu garuu Abbaa koo ti.
> *Jesus therefore said to them, “Most certainly, I tell you, it wasn’t Moses who gave you the bread out of heaven, but my Father gives you the true bread out of heaven.*
**33.** Buddeenni Waaqaa isa samii irraa gad buʼee addunyaaf jireenya kennu sanaatii.”
> *For the bread of God is that which comes down out of heaven and gives life to the world.”*
**34.** Isaanis, “Yaa Gooftaa, yeroo hunda buddeena kana nuu kenni” jedhaniin.
> *They said therefore to him, “Lord, always give us this bread.”*
**35.** Yesuusis akkana isaaniin jedhe; “Buddeenni jireenyaa ana. Namni gara koo dhufu gonkumaa hin beelaʼu; kan anatti amanus gonkumaa hin dheebotu.
> *Jesus said to them, “I am the bread of life. Whoever comes to me will not be hungry, and whoever believes in me will never be thirsty.*
**36.** Akkuma ani isinitti hime isin na argitaniirtu; garuu amma iyyuu hin amantan.
> *But I told you that you have seen me, and yet you don’t believe.*
**37.** Kan Abbaan naa kennu hundi gara koo ni dhufa; anis kan gara koo dhufu gonkumaa alatti hin ariʼu.
> *All those whom the Father gives me will come to me. He who comes to me I will in no way throw out.*
**38.** Ani fedhii isa na erge sanaa guutuuf malee, fedhii koo guutachuuf samii irraa gad hin buuneetii.
> *For I have come down from heaven, not to do my own will, but the will of him who sent me.*
**39.** Fedhiin isa na ergees akka ani warra inni natti kenne hunda guyyaa dhumaatti duʼaa kaasuuf malee akka ani isaan keessaa tokko illee balleessuuf miti.
> *This is the will of my Father who sent me, that of all he has given to me I should lose nothing, but should raise him up at the last day.*
**40.** Fedhiin Abbaa kootiis akka namni Ilma ilaalee isatti amanu hundi jireenya bara baraa qabaatuuf; anis guyyaa dhumaatti duʼaa isa nan kaasa.”
> *This is the will of the one who sent me, that everyone who sees the Son and believes in him should have eternal life; and I will raise him up at the last day.”*
**41.** Kana irratti Yihuudoonni sababii inni, “Ani buddeena samii irraa gad buʼee dha” jedheef isatti guungumuu jalqaban.
> *The Jews therefore murmured concerning him, because he said, “I am the bread which came down out of heaven.”*
**42.** Isaanis, “Kun Yesuus ilma Yoosef, isa nu abbaa fi haadha isaa beeknu sana mitii? Yoos inni akkamitti, ‘Ani samii irraa gad buʼeera’ jedha ree?” jedhan.
> *They said, “Isn’t this Jesus, the son of Joseph, whose father and mother we know? How then does he say, ‘I have come down out of heaven’?”*
**43.** Yesuus immoo akkana jedhee deebiseef; “Walitti hin guunguminaa.
> *Therefore Jesus answered them, “Don’t murmur among yourselves.*
**44.** Abbaan na erge sun isa harkisuu baannaan namni kam iyyuu gara koo dhufuu hin dandaʼu; anis guyyaa dhumaatti duʼaa isa nan kaasa.
> *No one can come to me unless the Father who sent me draws him; and I will raise him up in the last day.*
**45.** Raajota keessatti, ‘Isaan hundi warra Waaqa irraa baratan ni taʼu’ jedhamee barreeffameera. Namni Abbaa irraa dhagaʼee fi kan isa irraa barate hundi gara koo ni dhufa.
> *It is written in the prophets, ‘They will all be taught by God.’ Therefore everyone who hears from the Father and has learned, comes to me.*
**46.** Isa Waaqa biraa dhufe sana malee namni tokko iyyuu Abbaa hin argine; isa qofatu Abbaa arge.
> *Not that anyone has seen the Father, except he who is from God. He has seen the Father.*
**47.** Ani dhuguman isinitti hima; kan amanu jireenya bara baraa qaba.
> *Most certainly, I tell you, he who believes in me has eternal life.*
**48.** Buddeenni jireenyaa ana.
> *I am the bread of life.*
**49.** Abbootiin keessan gammoojjii keessatti mannaa nyaatan; taʼus ni duʼan.
> *Your fathers ate the manna in the wilderness and they died.*
**50.** Buddeenni samii irraa gad buʼe garuu isa kana; namni isa nyaatu gonkumaa hin duʼu.
> *This is the bread which comes down out of heaven, that anyone may eat of it and not die.*
**51.** Buddeenni jiraataan samii irraa buʼe ana. Namni kam iyyuu yoo buddeena kana irraa nyaate bara baraan ni jiraata. Buddeenni ani jireenya addunyaatiif kennus foon koo ti.”
> *I am the living bread which came down out of heaven. If anyone eats of this bread, he will live forever. Yes, the bread which I will give for the life of the world is my flesh.”*
**52.** Yihuudoonnis, “Namichi kun akkamitti akka nu nyaannuuf foon isaa nuu kennuu dandaʼa?” jedhanii wal falman.
> *The Jews therefore contended with one another, saying, “How can this man give us his flesh to eat?”*
**53.** Yesuus immoo akkana jedheen; “Ani dhuguman isinitti hima; isin foon Ilma Namaa nyaachuu baannaan, dhiiga isaas dhuguu baannaan ofii keessaniin jireenya hin qabdan.
> *Jesus therefore said to them, “Most certainly I tell you, unless you eat the flesh of the Son of Man and drink his blood, you don’t have life in yourselves.*
**54.** Namni foon koo nyaatuu fi dhiiga koo dhugu jireenya bara baraa qaba. Anis guyyaa dhumaatti duʼaa isa nan kaasa.
> *He who eats my flesh and drinks my blood has eternal life, and I will raise him up at the last day.*
**55.** Foon koo nyaata dhugaatii; dhiigni koos dhugaatii dhugaa ti.
> *For my flesh is food indeed, and my blood is drink indeed.*
**56.** Namni foon koo nyaatuu fi dhiiga koo dhugu hundi na keessa jiraata; anis isa keessa nan jiraadha.
> *He who eats my flesh and drinks my blood lives in me, and I in him.*
**57.** Akkuma Abbaan jiraataan na ergee anis sababii Abbaatiin jiraadhu sana, namni na nyaatus sababii kootiin ni jiraata.
> *As the living Father sent me, and I live because of the Father, so he who feeds on me will also live because of me.*
**58.** Buddeenni samii irraa buʼe isa kana; innis kan akka mannaa Abbootiin keessan nyaatanii duʼan sanaa miti; namni buddeena kana nyaatu bara baraan ni jiraata.”
> *This is the bread which came down out of heaven—not as our fathers ate the manna and died. He who eats this bread will live forever.”*
**59.** Innis Qifirnaahomitti utuu mana sagadaa keessatti barsiisaa jiruu waan kana dubbate.
> *He said these things in the synagogue, as he taught in Capernaum.*
**60.** Barattoota isaa keessaa baayʼeen yommuu waan kana dhagaʼanitti, “Barsiisni kun cimaa dha; eenyutu fudhachuu dandaʼa?” jedhan.
> *Therefore many of his disciples, when they heard this, said, “This is a hard saying! Who can listen to it?”*
**61.** Yesuusis akka barattoonni isaa waaʼee waan kanaa guunguman hubatee akkana jedheen; “Kun isin gufachiisaa ree?
> *But Jesus knowing in himself that his disciples murmured at this, said to them, “Does this cause you to stumble?*
**62.** Yoos yoo utuu Ilmi Namaa iddoo dur turetti ol baʼuu argitan akkam taʼinna ree?
> *Then what if you would see the Son of Man ascending to where he was before?*
**63.** Kan jireenya kennu Hafuura; foon faayidaa hin qabu. Dubbiin ani isinitti dubbadhe Hafuura; jireenyas.
> *It is the spirit who gives life. The flesh profits nothing. The words that I speak to you are spirit, and are life.*
**64.** Isin keessas warri hin amanne tokko tokko jiru.” Yesuus jalqabumaa kaasee isaan keessaa eenyu akka hin amaninii fi eenyu akka dabarsee isa kennu ni beeka tureetii.
> *But there are some of you who don’t believe.” For Jesus knew from the beginning who they were who didn’t believe, and who it was who would betray him.*
**65.** Innis ittuma fufee, “Sababiin ani, ‘Yoo Abbaa biraa isaaf kenname malee namni kam iyyuu gara koo dhufuu hin dandaʼu’ isiniin jedheefis kanuma” jedhe.
> *He said, “For this cause I have said to you that no one can come to me, unless it is given to him by my Father.”*
**66.** Yeroo kanaa jalqabee barattoota isaa keessaa baayʼeen duubatti deebiʼan; ergasiis isa wajjin hin deemne.
> *At this, many of his disciples went back and walked no more with him.*
**67.** Yesuusis warra Kudha Lamaaniin, “Isinis deemuu barbaadduu?” jedhee gaafate.
> *Jesus said therefore to the twelve, “You don’t also want to go away, do you?”*
**68.** Simoon Phexrosis akkana jedhee deebise; “Yaa Gooftaa, gara eenyuu deemna? Ati dubbii jireenya bara baraa qabdaa.
> *Simon Peter answered him, “Lord, to whom would we go? You have the words of eternal life.*
**69.** Nu akka ati Qulqullicha Waaqaa taate amanneerra; beekneerras.”
> *We have come to believe and know that you are the Christ, the Son of the living God.”*
**70.** Yesuusis deebisee, “Anumatu Kudha Lamaan keessan filate mitii? Taʼus isin keessaa inni tokko diiyaabiloosii dha!” jedhe.
> *Jesus answered them, “Didn’t I choose you, the twelve, and one of you is a devil?”*
**71.** Innis waaʼee Yihuudaa ilma Simoon Keeriyotichaa dubbate; Yihuudaan warra Kudha Lamaan keessaa tokko taʼus Yesuusin dabarsee kennuuf tureetii!
> *Now he spoke of Judas, the son of Simon Iscariot, for it was he who would betray him, being one of the twelve.*

## Boqonnaa 7 / Chapter 7

**1.** Kana booddee Yesuus Galiilaa keessa deddeebiʼaa ture; sababii Yihuudoonni isa ajjeesuu barbaadaniif inni Yihuudaa keessa hin deddeebine.
> *After these things, Jesus was walking in Galilee, for he wouldn’t walk in Judea, because the Jews sought to kill him.*

> *You are of your father the devil, and you want to do the desires of your father. He was a murderer from the beginning, and doesn’t stand in the truth, because there is no truth in him. When he speaks a lie, he speaks on his own; for he is a liar, and the father of lies.*
**45.** Isin garuu waan ani dhugaa dubbadhuuf na hin amantan!
> *But because I tell the truth, you don’t believe me.*
**46.** Isin keessaa namni cubbuudhaan na himatu eenyu? Erga ani dhugaa dubbadhee, isin maaliif na hin amanne?
> *Which of you convicts me of sin? If I tell the truth, why do you not believe me?*
**47.** Namni kan Waaqaa taʼe kam iyyuu dubbii Waaqaa ni dhagaʼa. Isin waan kan Waaqaa hin taʼiniif hin dhageessan.”
> *He who is of God hears the words of God. For this cause you don’t hear, because you are not of God.”*
**48.** Yihuudoonnis deebisanii, “Ati nama Samaariyaa ti; hafuura hamaatu si keessa jira jechuun keenya dhugaa mitii?” jedhaniin.
> *Then the Jews answered him, “Don’t we say well that you are a Samaritan, and have a demon?”*
**49.** Yesuus immoo akkana jedhe; “Hafuurri hamaan na keessa hin jiru; ani Abbaa koo nan kabaja; isin garuu na hin kabajjan.
> *Jesus answered, “I don’t have a demon, but I honor my Father and you dishonor me.*
**50.** Ani ulfina mataa koo hin barbaadu; garuu kan isa barbaadu tokkotu jira; innis Abbaa murtii ti.
> *But I don’t seek my own glory. There is one who seeks and judges.*
**51.** Ani dhuguman, dhuguman isinitti hima; eenyu iyyuu yoo dubbii koo eege bara baraan duʼa hin argu.”
> *Most certainly, I tell you, if a person keeps my word, he will never see death.”*
**52.** Yihuudoonnis akkana jedhaniin; “Akka hafuurri hamaan si keessa jiru nu amma barreerra. Abrahaam duʼeera; raajonnis duʼaniiru; ati garuu, ‘Eenyu iyyuu yoo dubbii koo eege bara baraan duʼa hin dhandhamu’ jetta.
> *Then the Jews said to him, “Now we know that you have a demon. Abraham died, as did the prophets; and you say, ‘If a man keeps my word, he will never taste of death.’*
**53.** Ati abbaa keenya Abrahaam caaltaa? Inni duʼeera; raajonnis duʼaniiru. Egaa ati eenyu ofiin jetta ree?”
> *Are you greater than our father Abraham, who died? The prophets died. Who do you make yourself out to be?”*
**54.** Yesuus akkana jedhee deebise; “Ani yoon ofii kootiif ulfina kenne, ulfinni koo gatii hin qabu. Kan ulfina naa kennu Abbaa koo isuma isin Waaqa keenya jettaniin sanaa dha.
> *Jesus answered, “If I glorify myself, my glory is nothing. It is my Father who glorifies me, of whom you say that he is our God.*
**55.** Yoo isin isa beekuu baattan illee ani isa beeka. Ani utuu isa hin beeku jedhee silaa akkuma keessan sobduu nan taʼa ture. Ani garuu isa beeka; dubbii isaa illee nan eega.
> *You have not known him, but I know him. If I said, ‘I don’t know him,’ I would be like you, a liar. But I know him and keep his word.*
**56.** Abbaan keessan Abrahaam guyyaa koo arguuf gammadee ture; innis ni arge; ni gammades.”
> *Your father Abraham rejoiced to see my day. He saw it and was glad.”*
**57.** Yihuudoonnis, “Umuriin kee waggaa shantama iyyuu hin guunne; yoos Abrahaamin argiteertaa?” jedhaniin.
> *The Jews therefore said to him, “You are not yet fifty years old! Have you seen Abraham?”*
**58.** Yesuusis, “Ani dhuguman, dhuguman isinitti hima; utuu Abrahaam hin dhalatin iyyuu ani anuma” isaaniin jedhe.
> *Jesus said to them, “Most certainly, I tell you, before Abraham came into existence, I AM.”*
**59.** Kana irratti isaan isa dhaʼuuf jedhanii dhagaa guurratan; Yesuus garuu dhokatee mana qulqullummaatii baʼee deeme.
> *Therefore they took up stones to throw at him, but Jesus hid himself and went out of the temple, having gone through the middle of them, and so passed by.*

## Boqonnaa 9 / Chapter 9

**1.** Yesuus utuu darbuu namicha dhalootuma isaatii jalqabee jaamaa ture tokko arge.
> *As he passed by, he saw a man blind from birth.*
**2.** Barattoonni isaas, “Yaa barsiisaa, akka namichi kun jaamaa taʼee dhalatuuf eenyutu cubbuu hojjete? Isuma moo abbaa fi haadha isaa ti?” jedhanii isa gaafatan.
> *His disciples asked him, “Rabbi, who sinned, this man or his parents, that he was born blind?”*
**3.** Yesuusis akkana jedhee deebise; “Akka hojiin Waaqaa isaan mulʼatuuf malee inni yookaan abbaa fi haati isaa cubbuu hojjetanii miti.
> *Jesus answered, “This man didn’t sin, nor did his parents, but that the works of God might be revealed in him.*
**4.** Utuu guyyaa taʼee jiruu hojii isa na ergee hojjechuun qabna. Halkan namni tokko iyyuu itti hojjechuu hin dandeenye ni dhufa.
> *I must work the works of him who sent me while it is day. The night is coming, when no one can work.*
**5.** Ani yommuun addunyaa irra jiru, ifa addunyaa ti.”
> *While I am in the world, I am the light of the world.”*
**6.** Innis erga waan kana dubbatee booddee lafatti tufee dhoqqee tolche; dhoqqee sanas ija namichaatti dibe.
> *When he had said this, he spat on the ground, made mud with the saliva, anointed the blind man’s eyes with the mud,*
**7.** Innis namicha sanaan, “Dhaqiitii Haroo Saliihoomitti dhiqadhu” jedhe. Saliihoom jechuun kan ergame jechuu dha. Namichis dhaqee dhiqate; argaas deebiʼee dhufe.
> *and said to him, “Go, wash in the pool of Siloam” (which means “Sent”). So he went away, washed, and came back seeing.*
**8.** Olloonni isaatii fi namoonni duraan utuu inni kadhatuu argan, “Namichi kun isuma taaʼee kadhachaa ture sana mitii?” jedhan.
> *Therefore the neighbors and those who saw that he was blind before said, “Isn’t this he who sat and begged?”*
**9.** Namoonni tokko tokko, “Eeyyee, isa” jedhan. Kaan immoo, “Miti, isa fakkaata malee” jedhan. Inni garuu, “Namichi sun anuma” jedhe.
> *Others were saying, “It is he.” Still others were saying, “He looks like him.” He said, “I am he.”*
**10.** Jarris, “Iji kee akkamitti baname ree?” jedhaniin.
> *They therefore were asking him, “How were your eyes opened?”*
**11.** Innis deebisee, “Namicha Yesuus jedhamutu dhoqqee tolchee ija koo dibee, ‘Gara Haroo Saliihoom dhaqiitii dhiqadhu’ naan jedhe; anis dhaqeen dhiqadhe; arguus nan dandaʼe.”
> *He answered, “A man called Jesus made mud, anointed my eyes, and said to me, ‘Go to the pool of Siloam and wash.’ So I went away and washed, and I received sight.”*
**12.** Isaanis, “Namichi sun eessa jira?” jedhanii isa gaafatan. Inni immoo, “Ani hin beeku” jedheen.
> *Then they asked him, “Where is he?” He said, “I don’t know.”*
**13.** Isaanis namicha jaamaa ture sana Fariisotatti geessan.
> *They brought him who had been blind to the Pharisees.*
**14.** Guyyaan itti Yesuus dhoqqee tolchee ija namichaa bane Sanbata ture.
> *It was a Sabbath when Jesus made the mud and opened his eyes.*
**15.** Fariisonnis namichi akkamitti akka arguu dandaʼe isa gaafatan; namichis, “Inni dhoqqee tolchee ija koo dibe; anis nan dhiqadhe; nan arges” jedheen.
> *Again therefore the Pharisees also asked him how he received his sight. He said to them, “He put mud on my eyes, I washed, and I see.”*
**16.** Fariisota keessaas tokko tokko, “Namichi kun waan Sanbata hin eegneef, inni Waaqa biraa hin dhufne” jedhan. Warri kaan garuu, “Cubbamaan tokko akkamitti dinqii akkanaa hojjechuu dandaʼa?” jedhan. Garaa garummaanis isaan gidduutti uumame.
> *Some therefore of the Pharisees said, “This man is not from God, because he doesn’t keep the Sabbath.” Others said, “How can a man who is a sinner do such signs?” So there was division among them.*
**17.** Isaanis amma illee namicha jaamaa ture sanaan, “Waaʼee namicha ija kee siif bane sanaa maal jetta?” jedhan. Namichis deebisee, “Inni raajii dha” jedhe.
> *Therefore they asked the blind man again, “What do you say about him, because he opened your eyes?” He said, “He is a prophet.”*
**18.** Yihuudoonnis hamma abbaa fi haadha namicha agartuu isaa argate sanaa waamanitti akka inni jaamaa turee fi akka inni agartuu isaa argate amma illee hin amanne.
> *The Jews therefore didn’t believe concerning him, that he had been blind and had received his sight, until they called the parents of him who had received his sight,*
**19.** Isaanis, “Inni kun ilma keessan isuma isin jaamaa taʼee dhalate jettan sanaa? Yoos amma immoo akkamitti arguu dandaʼa ree?” jedhanii gaafatan.
> *and asked them, “Is this your son, whom you say was born blind? How then does he now see?”*
**20.** Abbaa fi haati namichaas deebisanii akkana jedhan; “Akka inni ilma keenya taʼee fi akka inni jaamaa taʼee dhalate ni beekna.
> *His parents answered them, “We know that this is our son, and that he was born blind;*
**21.** Garuu amma akkamitti akka arguu dandaʼe yookaan ija isaa eenyu akka baneef nu hin beeknu. Inni nama gaʼaa dha; waaʼee mataa isaa dubbachuu ni dandaʼa; isuma gaafadhaa.”
> *but how he now sees, we don’t know; or who opened his eyes, we don’t know. He is of age. Ask him. He will speak for himself.”*
**22.** Abbaa fi haati isaa sababii Yihuudoota sodaataniif waan kana jedhan; namni kam iyyuu yoo Yesuus inni Kiristoos jedhee dhugaa baʼe akka mana sagadaa keessaa ariʼamu Yihuudoonni kanaan dura walii galanii murteessanii turaniitii.
> *His parents said these things because they feared the Jews; for the Jews had already agreed that if any man would confess him as Christ, he would be put out of the synagogue.*
**23.** Kanaafuu abbaa fi haati isaa, “Inni nama gaʼaa dha; isuma gaafadhaa” jedhan.
> *Therefore his parents said, “He is of age. Ask him.”*
**24.** Isaanis yeroo lammaffaa namicha jaamaa ture sana waamanii, “Waaqaaf ulfina kenni. Namichi kun cubbamaa taʼuu isaa nu ni beekna” jedhaniin.
> *So they called the man who was blind a second time, and said to him, “Give glory to God. We know that this man is a sinner.”*
**25.** Innis, “Cubbamaa taʼuu fi taʼuu baachuu isaa ani hin beeku. Ani waan tokkon beeka. Ani jaamaan ture; amma garuu nan arga!” jedhee deebise.
> *He therefore answered, “I don’t know if he is a sinner. One thing I do know: that though I was blind, now I see.”*
**26.** Isaanis, “Inni maal siif godhe? Ija kees akkamitti siif bane?” jedhanii isa gaafatan.
> *They said to him again, “What did he do to you? How did he open your eyes?”*
**27.** Inni immoo, “Ani kanaan dura isinitti himeera; isinis hin dhageenye. Ammas maaliif dhagaʼuu barbaaddu? Isinis barattoota isaa taʼuu barbaadduu?” jedheen.
> *He answered them, “I told you already, and you didn’t listen. Why do you want to hear it again? You don’t also want to become his disciples, do you?”*
**28.** Isaanis akkana jedhanii isa arrabsan; “Barataan namicha kanaa siʼi! Nu barattoota Musee ti!
> *They insulted him and said, “You are his disciple, but we are disciples of Moses.*
**29.** Akka Waaqni Museetti dubbate nu beekna; namichi kun garuu eessaa akka dhufe iyyuu hin beeknu.”
> *We know that God has spoken to Moses. But as for this man, we don’t know where he comes from.”*
**30.** Namichis deebisee akkana jedhe; “Inni eessaa akka dhufe wallaaluun keessan waan nama dinquu dha; garuu inni ija koo naa bane.
> *The man answered them, “How amazing! You don’t know where he comes from, yet he opened my eyes.*
**31.** Akka Waaqni cubbamoota hin dhageenye ni beekna. Inni nama isa waaqeffatuu fi fedhii isaa guutu dhagaʼa.
> *We know that God doesn’t listen to sinners, but if anyone is a worshiper of God and does his will, he listens to him.*
**32.** Eenyu iyyuu ija nama jaamaa taʼee dhalate tokkoo akka bane uumama addunyaatii jalqabee hin dhagaʼamne.
> *Since the world began it has never been heard of that anyone opened the eyes of someone born blind.*
**33.** Namichi kun utuu Waaqa biraa taʼuu baatee silaa homaa gochuu hin dandaʼu ture.”
> *If this man were not from God, he could do nothing.”*
**34.** Isaanis deebisanii, “Ati kan guutummaatti cubbuun dhalatte, situ nu barsiisaa?” jedhaniin. Alattis gad isa baasan.
> *They answered him, “You were altogether born in sins, and do you teach us?” Then they threw him out.*
**35.** Yesuusis akka isaan gad isa baasan dhagaʼe; yommuu isa argettis, “Ati Ilma Namaatti ni amantaa?” jedheen.
> *Jesus heard that they had thrown him out, and finding him, he said, “Do you believe in the Son of God?”*
**36.** Namichis, “Yaa gooftaa, akka ani isatti amanuuf inni eenyu?” jedhe.
> *He answered, “Who is he, Lord, that I may believe in him?”*
**37.** Yesuusis, “Ati amma isa argiteerta; inni si wajjin haasaʼaa jiru isuma” jedheen.
> *Jesus said to him, “You have both seen him, and it is he who speaks with you.”*
**38.** Namichi sunis, “Yaa Gooftaa, ani nan amana” jedhe; ni sagadeefis.
> *He said, “Lord, I believe!” and he worshiped him.*
**39.** Yesuus immoo, “Warri hin argine akka arganiif, warri argan immoo akka jaamaniif ani murtiif gara addunyaa kanaa dhufeera” jedhe.
> *Jesus said, “I came into this world for judgment, that those who don’t see may see; and that those who see may become blind.”*
**40.** Fariisota isa wajjin turan keessaa tokko tokko waan kana jechuu isaa dhagaʼanii, “Nus jaamota mitii ree?” jedhanii gaafatan.
> *Those of the Pharisees who were with him heard these things, and said to him, “Are we also blind?”*
**41.** Yesuusis, “Utuu jaamota taatanii silaa cubbuu hin qabdan ture; isin garuu, ‘Ni argina’ jettu; cubbuun keessan ni jiraata” jedheen.
> *Jesus said to them, “If you were blind, you would have no sin; but now you say, ‘We see.’ Therefore your sin remains.*

## Boqonnaa 10 / Chapter 10

**1.** “Ani dhuguman, dhuguman isinitti hima; namni balbalaan gola hoolotaa hin seennee, garuu karaa biraatiin koree seenu inni hattuu fi saamtuu dha.
> *“Most certainly, I tell you, one who doesn’t enter by the door into the sheep fold, but climbs up some other way, is a thief and a robber.*
**2.** Namni balbalaan seenu immoo tiksee hoolotaa ti.
> *But one who enters in by the door is the shepherd of the sheep.*
**3.** Eegduunis balbala isaaf bana; hoolonnis sagalee isaa ni dhagaʼu. Innis hoolota isaa maqaa maqaan waamee gad yaasa.
> *The gatekeeper opens the gate for him, and the sheep listen to his voice. He calls his own sheep by name and leads them out.*
**4.** Erga warra kan isaa taʼan hunda gad yaasee booddee isaan dura deema; hoolonni isaas waan sagalee isaa beekaniif isa faana buʼu.
> *Whenever he brings out his own sheep, he goes before them; and the sheep follow him, for they know his voice.*
**5.** Orma garuu ni baqatu malee faana hin buʼan; sagalee ormaa hin beekaniitii.”
> *They will by no means follow a stranger, but will flee from him; for they don’t know the voice of strangers.”*
**6.** Yesuus fakkeenya kana isaanitti hime; isaan garuu akka inni maal isaanitti himaa ture hin hubanne.
> *Jesus spoke this parable to them, but they didn’t understand what he was telling them.*
**7.** Kanaafuu Yesuus ammas akkana jedheen; “Dhuguman, dhuguman isinitti hima; ani balbala hoolotaa ti.
> *Jesus therefore said to them again, “Most certainly, I tell you, I am the sheep’s door.*
**8.** Warri na dura dhufan hundi hattootaa fi saamtota; hoolonni garuu isaan hin dhageenye.
> *All who came before me are thieves and robbers, but the sheep didn’t listen to them.*
**9.** Balballi ana; namni karaa kootiin ol seenu kam iyyuu ni fayya. Inni ni seena; ni baʼas; lafa dheedaas ni argata.
> *I am the door. If anyone enters in by me, he will be saved, and will go in and go out and will find pasture.*
**10.** Hattichi hatuuf, ajjeesuu fi balleessuu qofaaf dhufa; ani garuu akka isaan jireenya qabaataniif, jireenya irraa hafaas akka qabaataniif dhufeera.
> *The thief only comes to steal, kill, and destroy. I came that they may have life, and may have it abundantly.*
**11.** “Tikseen gaariin ana. Tikseen gaariin hoolota isaatiif jedhee lubbuu isaa dabarsee kenna.
> *“I am the good shepherd. The good shepherd lays down his life for the sheep.*
**12.** Namni qacaramaan kan tiksee hin taʼin garuu waan hoolonni kan isaa hin taʼiniif yommuu dhufaatii yeeyyii argutti hoolota dhiisee baqata. Yeeyyiinis bushaayee ni butata; ni bittinneessas.
> *He who is a hired hand, and not a shepherd, who doesn’t own the sheep, sees the wolf coming, leaves the sheep, and flees. The wolf snatches the sheep and scatters them.*
**13.** Namichi waan qacaramaa taʼeef ni baqata; dhimma hoolotaas hin qabu.
> *The hired hand flees because he is a hired hand and doesn’t care for the sheep.*
**14.** “Tikseen gaariin ana; ani hoolota koo beeka; hoolonni koos na beeku;
> *I am the good shepherd. I know my own, and I’m known by my own;*
**15.** kunis akkuma Abbaan na beekuttii fi akkuma ani Abbaa beekutti. Anis lubbuu koo hoolotaaf nan kenna.
> *even as the Father knows me, and I know the Father. I lay down my life for the sheep.*
**16.** Ani warra gola kanatti galan malees hoolota gara biraan qaba. Isaanis fiduun qaba. Isaanis sagalee koo ni dhagaʼu; karra tokkos ni taʼu; tikseen isaaniis tokko taʼa.
> *I have other sheep which are not of this fold. I must bring them also, and they will hear my voice. They will become one flock with one shepherd.*
**17.** Kanaafuu Abbaan waan ani deebisee fudhachuuf lubbuu koo dabarsee kennuuf na jaallata.
> *Therefore the Father loves me, because I lay down my life, that I may take it again.*
**18.** Lubbuu koo anumatu ofii kootiin dabarsee kenna malee eenyu iyyuu narraa hin fudhatu. Ani lubbuu koo dabarsee kennuuf taayitaan qaba; deebisee fudhachuufis taayitaan qaba. Ajaja kanas Abbaa koo biraa fudhadheera.”
> *No one takes it away from me, but I lay it down by myself. I have power to lay it down, and I have power to take it again. I received this commandment from my Father.”*
**19.** Amma illee sababii dubbii kanaatiif Yihuudoota gidduutti garaa garummaan uumame.
> *Therefore a division arose again among the Jews because of these words.*
**20.** Baayʼeen isaaniis, “Hafuura hamaatu isa keessa jira; inni maraateera. Isin maaliif isa dhageessu?” jedhan.
> *Many of them said, “He has a demon and is insane! Why do you listen to him?”*
**21.** Warri kaan immoo, “Kun jecha nama hafuura hamaadhaan qabame miti. Hafuurri hamaan ija nama jaamaa banuu ni dandaʼaa?” jedhan.
> *Others said, “These are not the sayings of one possessed by a demon. It isn’t possible for a demon to open the eyes of the blind, is it?”*
**22.** Yeroo sana Ayyaanni mana qulqullummaa haaromsuu Yerusaalemitti ayyaaneffamee ture. Yeroon sunis ganna ture;
> *It was the Feast of the Dedication at Jerusalem.*
**23.** Yesuusis mana qulqullummaa keessa gardaafoo Solomoon keessa deddeebiʼaa ture.
> *It was winter, and Jesus was walking in the temple, in Solomon’s porch.*
**24.** Yihuudoonnis isa marsanii, “Hamma yoomiitti qalbii nu rarraaftaa? Ati yoo Kiristoos taate ifatti baasii nutti himi” jedhaniin.
> *The Jews therefore came around him and said to him, “How long will you hold us in suspense? If you are the Christ, tell us plainly.”*
**25.** Yesuusis akkana jedhee deebiseef; “Ani isinitti himeera; isin garuu hin amantan. Hojiin ani maqaa Abbaa kootiitiin hojjedhu waaʼee koo dhugaa baʼa.
> *Jesus answered them, “I told you, and you don’t believe. The works that I do in my Father’s name, these testify about me.*
**26.** Isin garuu waan hoolota koo hin taʼiniif hin amantan.
> *But you don’t believe, because you are not of my sheep, as I told you.*
**27.** Hoolonni koo sagalee koo ni dhagaʼu; anis isaan beeka; isaanis na faana buʼu.
> *My sheep hear my voice, and I know them, and they follow me.*
**28.** Anis jireenya bara baraa nan kennaaf; isaanis gonkumaa hin badan. Eenyu iyyuu harka koo keessaa isaan hin butatu.
> *I give eternal life to them. They will never perish, and no one will snatch them out of my hand.*
**29.** Abbaan koo kan natti isaan kenne sun hunduma caala; namni tokko iyyuu harka Abbaa kootii keessaa isaan butachuu hin dandaʼu.
> *My Father who has given them to me is greater than all. No one is able to snatch them out of my Father’s hand.*
**30.** Anii fi Abbaan tokkuma.”
> *I and the Father are one.”*
**31.** Yihuudoonni isa dhaʼuuf jedhanii ammas dhagaa guurratan.
> *Therefore the Jews took up stones again to stone him.*
**32.** Yesuusis, “Ani hojii Abbaa kootii gaarii baayʼee isinitti argisiiseera. Hojii kana keessaa isin sababii isa kamiitiif dhagaadhaan na dhooftu?” jedheen.
> *Jesus answered them, “I have shown you many good works from my Father. For which of those works do you stone me?”*
**33.** Yihuudoonnis, “Nu hojii gaariif jennee dhagaadhaan si hin dhoofnu; garuu waan ati Waaqa arrabsitee utumaa nama taatee jirtuu Waaqa of gooteef si dhoofna” jedhanii deebisaniif.
> *The Jews answered him, “We don’t stone you for a good work, but for blasphemy, because you, being a man, make yourself God.”*
**34.** Yesuus immoo akkana jedhee deebiseef; “Seera keessan keessatti, ‘Anuu isin, “waaqota” jedheera’ jedhamee barreeffameera mitii?
> *Jesus answered them, “Isn’t it written in your law, ‘I said, you are gods’?*
**35.** Inni erga warra dubbiin Waaqaa isaaniif dhufeen ‘waaqota’ jedhee, Katabbiin Qulqulluun immoo waan hin diigamneef,
> *If he called them gods, to whom the word of God came (and the Scripture can’t be broken),*
**36.** sababii ani, ‘Ani ilma Waaqaa ti’ jedheef, isin maaliif isa Abbaan qulqulleessee gara addunyaatti erge sanaan ‘Ati Waaqa arrabsita’ jettu?
> *do you say of him whom the Father sanctified and sent into the world, ‘You blaspheme,’ because I said, ‘I am the Son of God’?*
**37.** Yoo ani hojii Abbaa kootii hojjechuu baadhe na hin amaninaa.
> *If I don’t do the works of my Father, don’t believe me.*
**38.** Yoo ani hojii sana hojjedhe garuu yoo na amanuu baattan illee hojicha amanaa; kunis akka Abbaan na keessa jiru, akka anis Abbaa keessa jiru beektanii akka hubattaniif.”
> *But if I do them, though you don’t believe me, believe the works, that you may know and believe that the Father is in me, and I in the Father.”*

> *The chief priests therefore and the Pharisees gathered a council, and said, “What are we doing? For this man does many signs.*

## Boqonnaa 12 / Chapter 12

**33.** Innis akka duʼa akkamii duʼuuf jiru argisiisuuf kana jedhe.
> *But he said this, signifying by what kind of death he should die.*
**34.** Tuunnis deebisee, “Akka Kiristoos bara baraan jiraatu nu Seera irraa dhageenyeerra; yoos ati akkamitti, ‘Ilmi Namaa ol fuudhamuu qaba’ jetta? ‘Ilmi Namaa’ kunis eenyu?” jedheen.
> *The multitude answered him, “We have heard out of the law that the Christ remains forever. How do you say, ‘The Son of Man must be lifted up’? Who is this Son of Man?”*
**35.** Yesuus immoo akkana jedheen; “Ifni yeroo gabaabaadhaaf isin bira jira. Akka dukkanni isin hin liqimsineef utuu ifa qabdanuu deddeebiʼaa. Namni dukkana keessa deddeebiʼu eessa akka dhaqu hin beekuutii.
> *Jesus therefore said to them, “Yet a little while the light is with you. Walk while you have the light, that darkness doesn’t overtake you. He who walks in the darkness doesn’t know where he is going.*
**36.** Ijoollee ifaa akka taataniif utuu ifa qabdanuu ifa sanatti amanaa.” Yesuusis erga waan kana dubbatee fixee booddee iddoo sanaa kaʼee isaan duraa dhokate.
> *While you have the light, believe in the light, that you may become children of light.” Jesus said these things, and he departed and hid himself from them.*
**37.** Yesuus fuula isaanii duratti mallattoo hedduu hojjetu iyyuu, isaan isatti hin amanne.
> *But though he had done so many signs before them, yet they didn’t believe in him,*
**38.** Kunis dubbiin Isaayyaas raajichaa kan: “Yaa Gooftaa, ergaa keenya eenyutu amane? Irreen Gooftaas eenyutti mulʼate?” jedhu sun akka raawwatamuuf ture.
> *that the word of Isaiah the prophet might be fulfilled, which he spoke: “Lord, who has believed our report? To whom has the arm of the Lord been revealed?”*
**39.** Sababii kanaaf isaan amanuu hin dandeenye; Isaayyaas iddoo biraatti akkana jedheeraatii;
> *For this cause they couldn’t believe, for Isaiah said again:*
**40.** “Ija isaaniitiin akka hin argineef, qalbii isaaniitiin akka hin hubanneef, akka ani isaan fayyisuufis akka gara kootti hin deebineef, inni ija isaanii jaamseeraatii; garaa isaaniis jabeesseera.”
> *“He has blinded their eyes and he hardened their heart, lest they should see with their eyes, and perceive with their heart, and would turn, and I would heal them.”*
**41.** Isaayyaas sababii ulfina isaa argee waaʼee isaas dubbateef waan kana jedhe.
> *Isaiah said these things when he saw his glory, and spoke of him.*
**42.** Taʼus hooggantoota keessaa iyyuu namoonni baayʼeen isatti amanan. Garuu waan mana sagadaatii nu baasu jedhanii Fariisota sodaataniif amantii isaanii ifatti hin baafne;
> *Nevertheless, even many of the rulers believed in him, but because of the Pharisees they didn’t confess it, so that they wouldn’t be put out of the synagogue,*
**43.** isaan ulfina Waaqaa irra ulfina namaa jaallataniiruutii.
> *for they loved men’s praise more than God’s praise.*
**44.** Yesuusis iyyee akkana jedhe; “Namni natti amanu isa na erge sanatti amana malee natti miti.
> *Jesus cried out and said, “Whoever believes in me, believes not in me, but in him who sent me.*
**45.** Kan na argus isa na erge sana arga.
> *He who sees me sees him who sent me.*
**46.** Akka namni natti amanu hundinuu dukkana keessa hin jiraanneef ani ifa taʼee gara addunyaa dhufeera.
> *I have come as a light into the world, that whoever believes in me may not remain in the darkness.*
**47.** “Namni yoo dubbii koo dhagaʼee eeguu baate kan isatti muru ana miti; ani addunyaa fayyisuufin dhufe malee itti muruuf hin dhufneetii.
> *If anyone listens to my sayings and doesn’t believe, I don’t judge him. For I came not to judge the world, but to save the world.*
**48.** Namni na didu kan dubbii koos hin fudhanne abbaa murtii tokko qaba; dubbiin ani dubbadhe sun guyyaa dhumaatti isatti mura.
> *He who rejects me, and doesn’t receive my sayings, has one who judges him. The word that I spoke will judge him in the last day.*
**49.** Garuu Abbaa isuma na erge sanatu akka ani waanan dubbadhe sana jedhu na ajaje malee ani ofii kootiin hin dubbanne.
> *For I spoke not from myself, but the Father who sent me gave me a commandment, what I should say and what I should speak.*
**50.** Akka ajajni isaa jireenya bara baraa taʼe nan beeka. Kanaafuu wanni ani dubbadhu kam iyyuu waan akka ani dubbadhuuf Abbaan natti himee dha.”
> *I know that his commandment is eternal life. The things therefore which I speak, even as the Father has said to me, so I speak.”*

## Boqonnaa 13 / Chapter 13

**1.** Yeroon sun Ayyaana Faasiikaatiin dura ture. Yesuusis akka addunyaa kana keessaa gara Abbaa dhaquudhaaf saʼaatiin isaa gaʼe ni beeke. Innis warra kan isaa taʼan kanneen addunyaa keessa turan ni jaallate; hamma dhumaattis isaan jaallate.
> *Now before the feast of the Passover, Jesus, knowing that his time had come that he would depart from this world to the Father, having loved his own who were in the world, he loved them to the end.*
**2.** Yeroo irbaataatti diiyaabiloos akka inni Yesuusin dabarsee kennuuf Yihuudaa Keeriyoticha ilma Simoon keessa duraan dursee yaada kaaʼe.
> *During supper, the devil having already put into the heart of Judas Iscariot, Simon’s son, to betray him,*
**3.** Yesuusis akka Abbaan waan hunda harka isaatti kenne, akka Waaqa biraa dhufee fi akka gara Waaqaatti deebiʼu illee beeka ture;
> *Jesus, knowing that the Father had given all things into his hands, and that he came from God and was going to God,*
**4.** kanaaf irbaata irraa kaʼee, wayyaa isaa kan irraan uffate of irraa fuudhee lafa kaaʼe; fooxaas mudhiitti hidhate.
> *arose from supper, and laid aside his outer garments. He took a towel and wrapped a towel around his waist.*
**5.** Ergasii gabateetti bishaan naqatee miilla barattootaa dhiquu fi fooxaa mudhiitti hidhate sanaanis haquu jalqabe.
> *Then he poured water into the basin, and began to wash the disciples’ feet and to wipe them with the towel that was wrapped around him.*
**6.** Inni gara Simoon Phexros dhufe; Phexrosis, “Yaa Gooftaa, situ miilla koo naa dhiqaa?” jedheen.
> *Then he came to Simon Peter. He said to him, “Lord, do you wash my feet?”*
**7.** Yesuus immoo deebisee, “Waan ani hojjechaa jiru ati amma hin beektu; booddee garuu ni hubatta” jedheen.
> *Jesus answered him, “You don’t know what I am doing now, but you will understand later.”*
**8.** Phexrosis, “Hin taʼu; ati gonkumaa miilla koo naa hin dhiqxu” jedheen. Yesuusis deebisee, “Yoo ani siif dhiquu baadhe ati na biraa qooda hin qabaattu” jedheen.
> *Peter said to him, “You will never wash my feet!” Jesus answered him, “If I don’t wash you, you have no part with me.”*
**9.** Simoon Phexrosis deebisee, “Yaa Gooftaa, yoos harka koo fi mataa koos malee miilla koo qofa hin taʼin” jedheen.
> *Simon Peter said to him, “Lord, not my feet only, but also my hands and my head!”*
**10.** Yesuusis, “Namni dhagna isaa dhiqate tokko miilla isaa qofa dhiqachuu isa barbaachisa; dhagni isaa guutuun qulqulluu dha. Isin qulqulluu dha; garuu hunda keessan miti” jedheen.
> *Jesus said to him, “Someone who has bathed only needs to have his feet washed, but is completely clean. You are clean, but not all of you.”*
**11.** Innis nama dabarsee isa kennuuf jiru beeka tureetii; sababiin inni, “Qulqulluun hunda keessan miti” jedheefis kanuma.
> *For he knew him who would betray him; therefore he said, “You are not all clean.”*
**12.** Innis erga miilla isaanii dhiqee booddee, uffata isaa uffatee iddoo isaatti deebiʼee taaʼe. Akkanas jedheen; “Isin waan ani isiniif godhe hubattanii?
> *So when he had washed their feet, put his outer garment back on, and sat down again, he said to them, “Do you know what I have done to you?*
**13.** Isin, ‘Barsiisaa’, ‘Gooftaas’ naan jettu; anis waan isuma isin jettan taʼeef isin dhuguma jettan.
> *You call me, ‘Teacher’ and ‘Lord.’ You say so correctly, for so I am.*
**14.** Yoos erga ani Gooftaa fi Barsiisaan keessan miilla keessan isinii dhiqee, isinis akkasuma miilla walii dhiquu qabdu.
> *If I then, the Lord and the Teacher, have washed your feet, you also ought to wash one another’s feet.*
**15.** Akkuma ani isinii godhe kana isinis akka gootaniif ani fakkeenya isinii kenneera.
> *For I have given you an example, that you should also do as I have done to you.*
**16.** Ani dhuguman, dhuguman isinitti hima; garbichi gooftaa isaa hin caalu; ergamaanis kan isa erge hin caalu.
> *Most certainly I tell you, a servant is not greater than his lord, neither is one who is sent greater than he who sent him.*
**17.** Egaa isin yoo waan kana beektanii hojii irra oolchitan isin eebbifamoo dha.
> *If you know these things, blessed are you if you do them.*
**18.** “Ani waaʼee hunda keessanii dubbachaa hin jiru; ani warran filadhe nan beeka. Kun garuu dubbiin Katabbii Qulqulluu kan, ‘Inni buddeena koo nyaate koomee isaa natti ol fudhate’ jedhu sun akka guutamuuf.
> *I don’t speak concerning all of you. I know whom I have chosen; but that the Scripture may be fulfilled, ‘He who eats bread with me has lifted up his heel against me.’*
**19.** “Isin yommuu wanni kun taʼutti akka ani Isa taʼe akka amantaniif utuu inni hin taʼin amma isinitti nan hima.
> *From now on, I tell you before it happens, that when it happens, you may believe that I am he.*
**20.** Ani dhuguman, dhuguman isinitti hima; kan nama ani ergu kam iyyuu simatu hundinuu na simata; kan na simatu hundis isa na erge sana simata.”
> *Most certainly I tell you, he who receives whomever I send, receives me; and he who receives me, receives him who sent me.”*
**21.** Yesuusis erga kana dubbatee booddee hafuuraan jeeqamee, “Ani dhuguman, dhuguman isinitti hima; isin keessaa tokko dabarsee na kenna” jedhee dhugaa baʼe.
> *When Jesus had said this, he was troubled in spirit, and testified, “Most certainly I tell you that one of you will betray me.”*
**22.** Barattoonni akka inni waaʼee eenyuu dubbate wallaalanii wal ilaalan.
> *The disciples looked at one another, perplexed about whom he spoke.*
**23.** Barattoota keessaas inni Yesuus isa jaallatu tokko Yesuusitti irkatee maaddiitti dhiʼaatee ture.
> *One of his disciples, whom Jesus loved, was at the table, leaning against Jesus’ chest.*
**24.** Simoon Phexrosis, “Mee eenyuu jechuu isaa akka taʼe isa gaafadhu!” jedhee nyaaraan barataa sanatti hime.
> *Simon Peter therefore beckoned to him, and said to him, “Tell us who it is of whom he speaks.”*
**25.** Innis qoma Yesuusiitti hirkatee, “Yaa Gooftaa, eenyu innii?” jedhee gaafate.
> *He, leaning back, as he was, on Jesus’ chest, asked him, “Lord, who is it?”*
**26.** Yesuusis deebisee, “Namichi ani buddeena afaan kaaʼata tokkoo caabii keessa cuuphee kennuuf isa” jedhe. Innis buddeena caabii keessa cuuphee Yihuudaa ilma Simoon Keeriyotichaatiif kenne.
> *Jesus therefore answered, “It is he to whom I will give this piece of bread when I have dipped it.” So when he had dipped the piece of bread, he gave it to Judas, the son of Simon Iscariot.*
**27.** Yihuudaas akkuma inni buddeenicha fudhateen Seexanni itti gale. Yesuusis, “Waan gochuuf jirtu dafii godhi” isaan jedhe.
> *After the piece of bread, then Satan entered into him. Then Jesus said to him, “What you do, do quickly.”*
**28.** Warra nyaataaf tataaʼan keessaas namni tokko iyyuu waan inni akkas isaan jedheef hin hubanne.
> *Now nobody at the table knew why he said this to him.*
**29.** Sababii Yihuudaan korojoo maallaqaa baachaa tureefis isaan keessaa namoonni tokko tokko waan Yesuus, akka inni waan Ayyaanaaf barbaachisu bitu yookaan akka inni hiyyeeyyiif waa kennu isatti hime seʼan.
> *For some thought, because Judas had the money box, that Jesus said to him, “Buy what things we need for the feast,” or that he should give something to the poor.*
**30.** Yihuudaanis akkuma buddeena sana fudhateen gad baʼe. Yeroon sunis halkan ture.
> *Therefore having received that morsel, he went out immediately. It was night.*
**31.** Erga Yihuudaan gad baʼee booddee Yesuus akkana jedhe; “Amma Ilmi Namaa ulfaateera; Waaqnis isaan ulfina argateera.
> *When he had gone out, Jesus said, “Now the Son of Man has been glorified, and God has been glorified in him.*
**32.** Waaqni erga isaan ulfina argatee, Waaqni ofuma isaatiin Ilma ulfeessa; inni takkumaan isa ulfeessa.
> *If God has been glorified in him, God will also glorify him in himself, and he will glorify him immediately.*
**33.** “Yaa ijoolle, ani yeroo gabaabaaf isin wajjinan jira. Isin na barbaaddu; ani akkuma Yihuudootaan, ‘Iddoo ani dhaqu isin dhufuu hin dandeessan’ jedhe sana ammas isiniin nan jedha.
> *Little children, I will be with you a little while longer. You will seek me, and as I said to the Jews, ‘Where I am going, you can’t come,’ so now I tell you.*
**34.** “Akka isin wal jaallattaniif ani ajaja haaraa isiniifin kenna. Akkuma ani isin jaalladhetti isinis akkasuma wal jaalladhaa.
> *A new commandment I give to you, that you love one another. Just as I have loved you, you also love one another.*
**35.** Yoo isin jaalala waliif qabaattan namoonni hundinuu akka isin barattoota koo taatan kanaan ni beeku.”
> *By this everyone will know that you are my disciples, if you have love for one another.”*
**36.** Simoon Phexrosis, “Yaa Gooftaa, eessa dhaqxa?” jedhee isa gaafate. Yesuusis, “Ati amma iddoo ani dhaqu na faana dhaquu hin dandeessu; gara booddeetti garuu na duukaa buuta” jedhee deebiseef.
> *Simon Peter said to him, “Lord, where are you going?” Jesus answered, “Where I am going, you can’t follow now, but you will follow afterwards.”*
**37.** Phexrosis, “Yaa Gooftaa, ani maaliifan amma si duukaa buʼuu hin dandeenye? Ani siif jedhee lubbuu koo nan kenna” jedheen.
> *Peter said to him, “Lord, why can’t I follow you now? I will lay down my life for you.”*
**38.** Yesuusis deebisee, “Ati dhuguma naaf jettee lubbuu kee ni kennitaa? Ani dhuguman, dhuguman sitti hima; ati utuu indaanqoon hin iyyin yeroo sadii na ganta” jedheen.
> *Jesus answered him, “Will you lay down your life for me? Most certainly I tell you, the rooster won’t crow until you have denied me three times.*

## Boqonnaa 14 / Chapter 14

**1.** “Garaan keessan hin raafamin. Waaqatti amanaa; nattis amanaa.
> *“Don’t let your heart be troubled. Believe in God. Believe also in me.*
**2.** Mana Abbaa koo keessa iddoo jireenyaa baayʼeetu jira; utuu akkana taʼuu baatee silaa isinittin hima ture. Ani iddoo isinii qopheessuuf achin dhaqaatii.
> *In my Father’s house are many homes. If it weren’t so, I would have told you. I am going to prepare a place for you.*
**3.** Anis yoon dhaqee iddoo isinii qopheesse, akka isinis iddoo ani jiru jiraattaniif deebiʼee dhufeen ofitti isin fudhadha.
> *If I go and prepare a place for you, I will come again and will receive you to myself; that where I am, you may be there also.*
**4.** Isinis karaa iddoo ani dhaquu ni beektu.”
> *You know where I go, and you know the way.”*
**5.** Toomaasis, “Yaa Gooftaa, ati eessa akka dhaqxu nu hin beeknu; yoos akkamitti karaa sana beekuu dandeenya ree?” jedheen.
> *Thomas said to him, “Lord, we don’t know where you are going. How can we know the way?”*
**6.** Yesuusis akkana jedhee deebiseef; “Karaa fi dhugaan, jireenyis anuma. Yoo anaan taʼe malee eenyu iyyuu gara Abbaa hin dhufu.
> *Jesus said to him, “I am the way, the truth, and the life. No one comes to the Father, except through me.*
**7.** Utuu na beektanii silaa Abbaa koos akkasuma ni beektu turtan; ammaa jalqabdaniis isa beektu; isas argitaniirtu.”
> *If you had known me, you would have known my Father also. From now on, you know him and have seen him.”*
**8.** Fiiliphoos, “Yaa Gooftaa, Abbaa nutti argisiisi; kun nu gaʼaatii” jedheen.
> *Philip said to him, “Lord, show us the Father, and that will be enough for us.”*
**9.** Yesuusis akkana isaan jedhe; “Yaa Fiiliphoos, ani yeroo dheeraa akkanaa isin wajjin jiraadhee, ati amma illee na hin beeknee? Namni na arge Abbaas argeera. Yoos ati akkamitti, ‘Abbaa nutti argisiisi’ jechuu dandeessa ree?
> *Jesus said to him, “Have I been with you such a long time, and do you not know me, Philip? He who has seen me has seen the Father. How do you say, ‘Show us the Father’?*
**10.** Akka ani Abbaa keessa jiru, Abbaanis akka na keessa jiru ati hin amantuu? Ani dubbii isinitti dubbadhu kana ofuma kootiin hin dubbadhu. Garuu Abbaa isa na keessa jiraatutu hojii isaa hojjeta.
> *Don’t you believe that I am in the Father, and the Father in me? The words that I tell you, I speak not from myself; but the Father who lives in me does his works.*
**11.** Akka ani Abbaa keessa jiru, Abbaanis akka na keessa jiru na amanaa; yookaan sababii hojii sanaatiif na amanaa.
> *Believe me that I am in the Father, and the Father in me; or else believe me for the very works’ sake.*
**12.** Ani dhuguman, dhuguman isinitti hima; namni natti amanu hojii ani hojjedhu ni hojjeta. Ani waanan gara Abbaa dhaquuf inni waan kana caalu iyyuu ni hojjeta.
> *Most certainly I tell you, he who believes in me, the works that I do, he will do also; and he will do greater works than these, because I am going to my Father.*
**13.** Akka Abbaan Ilmaan ulfina argatuuf ani waan isin maqaa kootiin kadhattan kam iyyuu nan raawwadha.
> *Whatever you will ask in my name, I will do it, that the Father may be glorified in the Son.*
**14.** Yoo isin maqaa kootiin waan kam iyyuu na kadhattan ani nan raawwadha.
> *If you will ask anything in my name, I will do it.*
**15.** “Isin yoo na jaallattan ajaja koo eegaa.
> *If you love me, keep my commandments.*
**16.** Ani Abbaa nan kadhadha; innis akka inni bara baraan isin wajjin jiraatuuf Jajjabeessaa biraa isinii kenna;
> *I will pray to the Father, and he will give you another Counselor, that he may be with you forever:*
**17.** inni Hafuura dhugaa ti. Addunyaan waan isa hin arginee fi waan isa hin beekneef isa fudhachuu hin dandaʼu. Isin garuu waan inni isin wajjin jiraatuuf, isin keessas waan jiruuf isa ni beektu.
> *the Spirit of truth, whom the world can’t receive, for it doesn’t see him and doesn’t know him. You know him, for he lives with you and will be in you.*
**18.** Ani akka ijoollee haadhaa fi abbaa hin qabneetti isin hin dhiisu; gara keessan nan dhufa.
> *I will not leave you orphans. I will come to you.*
**19.** Yeroo muraasa booddee addunyaan deebiʼee na hin argu; isin garuu na argitu. Sababii ani jiraadhuuf isinis ni jiraattu.
> *Yet a little while, and the world will see me no more; but you will see me. Because I live, you will live also.*
**20.** Akka ani Abbaa koo keessa jiru, akka isin na keessa jirtan akka anis isin keessa jiru gaafas ni beektu.
> *In that day you will know that I am in my Father, and you in me, and I in you.*
**21.** Namni ajajawwan koo qabu, isaanis kan eegu, inni na jaallata. Nama na jaallatu Abbaan koo ni jaallata; anis isa nan jaalladha; isattis of nan mulʼisa.”
> *One who has my commandments and keeps them, that person is one who loves me. One who loves me will be loved by my Father, and I will love him, and will reveal myself to him.”*
**22.** Yihuudaa Keeriyoticha utuu hin taʼin inni Yihuudaan kaan, “Yaa Gooftaa, ati maaliif addunyaatti utuu hin taʼin nutti of mulʼisuu barbaadda?” jedheen.
> *Judas (not Iscariot) said to him, “Lord, what has happened that you are about to reveal yourself to us, and not to the world?”*
**23.** Yesuusis akkana jedhee deebiseef; “Namni na jaallatu kam iyyuu dubbii koo ni eega. Abbaan koo isa ni jaallata; nus gara isaa ni dhufna; iddoo jireenyaas isa wajjin ni godhanna.
> *Jesus answered him, “If a man loves me, he will keep my word. My Father will love him, and we will come to him and make our home with him.*
**24.** Namni na hin jaallanne dubbii koo hin eegu. Dubbiin isin dhageessan kunis kan Abbaa na erge sanaati malee kan koo miti.
> *He who doesn’t love me doesn’t keep my words. The word which you hear isn’t mine, but the Father’s who sent me.*
**25.** “Ani utuman isin wajjin jiruu waan kana isinitti dubbadheera.
> *“I have said these things to you while still living with you.*
**26.** Jajjabeessaan sun, Hafuurri Qulqulluun kan Abbaan maqaa kootiin ergu garuu waan hunda isin barsiisa; waan ani isinitti hime hundas isin yaadachiisa.
> *But the Counselor, the Holy Spirit, whom the Father will send in my name, will teach you all things, and will remind you of all that I said to you.*
**27.** Ani nagaa isin biratti nan hambisa; nagaa koos isiniif nan kenna. Ani akka addunyaan kennutti isiniif hin kennu. Garaan keessan hin raafamin; hin sodaatinis.
> *Peace I leave with you. My peace I give to you; not as the world gives, I give to you. Don’t let your heart be troubled, neither let it be fearful.*
**28.** “Isin akka ani, ‘Ani nan deema; deebiʼees gara keessan nan dhufa’ jedhe dhageessaniirtu. Utuu na jaallattanii silaa gara Abbaa dhaquu kootti ni gammaddu turtan; Abbaan na caalaatii.
> *You heard how I told you, ‘I am going away, and I will come back to you.’ If you loved me, you would have rejoiced because I said ‘I am going to my Father;’ for the Father is greater than I.*
**29.** Akka yommuu wanni kun taʼutti isin amantaniif utuu wanni kun hin taʼin ani amma duraan dursee isinitti himeera.
> *Now I have told you before it happens so that when it happens, you may believe.*
**30.** Waan bulchaan addunyaa kanaa dhufuuf, ani siʼachi isin wajjin baayʼee hin dubbadhu; inni narraa homaa hin qabu;
> *I will no more speak much with you, for the prince of the world comes, and he has nothing in me.*
**31.** garuu akka ani Abbaa jaalladhu addunyaan akka beekuuf, ani akkuma Abbaan na ajajetti nan hojjedha. “Kaʼaa, asii ni deemnaa.
> *But that the world may know that I love the Father, and as the Father commanded me, even so I do. Arise, let’s go from here.*

## Boqonnaa 15 / Chapter 15

**1.** “Ani muka wayinii dhugaa ti; kan dhaabes Abbaa koo ti.
> *“I am the true vine, and my Father is the farmer.*
**2.** Inni damee koo kan ija hin naqanne hunda muree gata; damee ija naqatu immoo akka inni caalaatti naqatuuf irraa qora.
> *Every branch in me that doesn’t bear fruit, he takes away. Every branch that bears fruit, he prunes, that it may bear more fruit.*
**3.** Isin dubbii ani isinitti dubbadheen amma qulqulluu dha.
> *You are already pruned clean because of the word which I have spoken to you.*
**4.** Na keessa jiraadhaa; anis isin keessa nan jiraadhaa. Akkuma dameen yoo muka wayiniitti jiraate malee ofuma isaatiin ija naqachuu hin dandeenye, isinis yoo na keessa jiraattan malee ija naqachuu hin dandeessan.
> *Remain in me, and I in you. As the branch can’t bear fruit by itself unless it remains in the vine, so neither can you, unless you remain in me.*
**5.** “Ani muka wayinii ti; isini immoo dameewwan. Namni yoo na keessa jiraate, anis yoon isa keessa jiraadhe inni ija baayʼee naqata; isin narraa gargar baatanii homaa gochuu hin dandeessaniitii.
> *I am the vine. You are the branches. He who remains in me and I in him bears much fruit, for apart from me you can do nothing.*
**6.** Namni na keessa hin jiraanne akka damee gad gatamee goguu ti. Dameewwan akkanaa walitti qabamanii ibiddatti naqamu; ni gubamus.
> *If a man doesn’t remain in me, he is thrown out as a branch and is withered; and they gather them, throw them into the fire, and they are burned.*
**7.** Isin yoo na keessa jiraattan, dubbiin koos yoo isin keessa jiraate, waanuma feetan kadhadhaa; innis isiniif ni kennama.
> *If you remain in me, and my words remain in you, you will ask whatever you desire, and it will be done for you.*
**8.** Isin yoo ija baayʼee naqattanii fi yoo barattoota koo taatan, kanaan Abbaan koo ulfina argata.
> *“In this my Father is glorified, that you bear much fruit; and so you will be my disciples.*
**9.** “Akkuma Abbaan na jaallatetti anis akkasuma isin jaalladheera. Egaa jaalala koo keessa jiraadhaa.
> *Even as the Father has loved me, I also have loved you. Remain in my love.*
**10.** Akkuma ani ajajawwan Abbaa kootii eegee jaalala isaa keessa jiraadhu, isinis yoo ajajawwan koo eegdan jaalala koo keessa ni jiraattu.
> *If you keep my commandments, you will remain in my love, even as I have kept my Father’s commandments and remain in his love.*
**11.** Akka gammachuun koo isin keessa jiraatuu fi akka gammachuun keessanis guutuu taʼuuf ani waan kana isinitti himeera.
> *I have spoken these things to you, that my joy may remain in you, and that your joy may be made full.*
**12.** Ajajni koo isa kana: Akkuma ani isin jaalladhetti isinis wal jaalladhaa.
> *“This is my commandment, that you love one another, even as I have loved you.*
**13.** Eenyu iyyuu jaalala guddaa kan namni lubbuu isaa dabarsee michoota isaatiif kennu sana irra caalu hin qabu.
> *Greater love has no one than this, that someone lay down his life for his friends.*
**14.** Waan ani isin ajaje yoo raawwattan isin michoota koo ti.
> *You are my friends if you do whatever I command you.*
**15.** Garbichi waan gooftaan isaa hojjetu waan hin beekneef, ani siʼachi garboota isiniin hin jedhu. Qooda kanaa ani michoota isiniin jedheera; ani waanan Abbaa koo irraa dhagaʼe hundumaa isin beeksiseeraatii.
> *No longer do I call you servants, for the servant doesn’t know what his lord does. But I have called you friends, for everything that I heard from my Father, I have made known to you.*
**16.** Isinis akka dhaqxanii ija naqattaniif, iji keessanis akka jiraatuuf anatu isin filatee isin muude malee isin na hin filanne. Kunis akka Abbaan waan isin maqaa kootiin kadhattan kam iyyuu isinii kennuuf.
> *You didn’t choose me, but I chose you and appointed you, that you should go and bear fruit, and that your fruit should remain; that whatever you will ask of the Father in my name, he may give it to you.*
**17.** Ajajni koo isa kana: Wal jaalladhaa.
> *“I command these things to you, that you may love one another.*
**18.** “Yoo addunyaan isin jibbe, akka inni isin dura na jibbe beekaa.
> *If the world hates you, you know that it has hated me before it hated you.*
**19.** Utuu isin kan addunyaa taatanii silaa addunyaan kan ofii isaa ni jaallata ture. Amma garuu waan ani addunyaa keessaa isin filadheef isin kan addunyaa miti. Kanaafuu addunyaan isin jibba.
> *If you were of the world, the world would love its own. But because you are not of the world, since I chose you out of the world, therefore the world hates you.*
**20.** Dubbii ani, ‘Garbichi gooftaa isaa hin caalu’ jedhee isinitti hime sana yaadadhaa. Isaan erga na ariʼatanii, isinis ni ariʼatu. Yoo dubbii kootiif ajajaman, dubbii keessaniifis ni ajajamu.
> *Remember the word that I said to you: ‘A servant is not greater than his lord.’ If they persecuted me, they will also persecute you. If they kept my word, they will also keep yours.*
**21.** Isaan waan Isa na erge sana hin beekneef, sababii maqaa kootiitiif waan kana hunda isin irratti raawwatu.
> *But they will do all these things to you for my name’s sake, because they don’t know him who sent me.*
**22.** Utuu ani dhufee isaanitti himuu baadhee silaa cubbuu isaanitti hin taʼu ture. Amma garuu cubbuu isaaniitiif sababii dhiʼeeffatan hin qaban.
> *If I had not come and spoken to them, they would not have had sin; but now they have no excuse for their sin.*
**23.** Namni na jibbu Abbaa koos ni jibba.
> *He who hates me, hates my Father also.*
**24.** Utuu ani hojii namni biraa tokko iyyuu hin hojjetin gidduu isaaniitti hojjechuu baadhee silaa cubbuu isaanitti hin taʼu ture. Amma garuu utuma waan ani hojjedhe arganuu anas Abbaa koos jibbaniiru.
> *If I hadn’t done among them the works which no one else did, they wouldn’t have had sin. But now they have seen and also hated both me and my Father.*
**25.** Kunis akka dubbiin Seera isaanii keessatti, ‘Isaan akkasumaan na jibban’ jedhamee barreeffame sun raawwatamuuf taʼe.
> *But this happened so that the word may be fulfilled which was written in their law, ‘They hated me without a cause.’*
**26.** “Jajjabeessaan ani Abbaa biraa isinii ergu, jechuunis Hafuurri Dhugaa inni Abbaa keessaa baʼu sun yommuu dhufutti waaʼee koo dhugaa baʼa.
> *“When the Counselor has come, whom I will send to you from the Father, the Spirit of truth, who proceeds from the Father, he will testify about me.*
**27.** Isinis jalqabaa kaaftanii waan na wajjin turtaniif dhugaa baatu.
> *You will also testify, because you have been with me from the beginning.*

## Boqonnaa 16 / Chapter 16

**1.** “Ani akka isin hin gufanneef jedhee wantoota kanneen isinitti himeera.
> *“I have said these things to you so that you wouldn’t be caused to stumble.*
**2.** Isaan mana sagadaa keessaa isin ariʼu; dhugumaan yeroon itti namni isin ajjeesu kam iyyuu akka waan Waaqa tajaajiluutti yaadu tokko ni dhufa.
> *They will put you out of the synagogues. Yes, the time is coming that whoever kills you will think that he offers service to God.*
**3.** Isaanis waan Abbaa yookaan na hin beekiniif wantoota kanneen godhu.
> *They will do these things because they have not known the Father nor me.*
**4.** Anis akka isin yommuu yeroon sun gaʼutti isaan yaadattaniif wantoota kanneen isinitti himeera. Ani waanan isin wajjin tureef jalqabatti wantoota kanneen isinitti hin himne.
> *But I have told you these things so that when the time comes, you may remember that I told you about them. I didn’t tell you these things from the beginning, because I was with you.*
**5.** “Amma garuu ani gara isa na ergee nan dhaqa; taʼus isin keessaa namni, ‘Ati eessa dhaqxa?’ jedhee na gaafatu tokko iyyuu hin jiru.
> *But now I am going to him who sent me, and none of you asks me, ‘Where are you going?’*
**6.** Garuu waan ani wantoota kanneen isinitti himeef garaan keessan gaddaan guutameera.
> *But because I have told you these things, sorrow has filled your heart.*
**7.** Ani garuu dhuguman isinitti hima; deemuun koo buʼaa isiniif qaba; yoo ani deeme malee Jajjabeessaan sun gara keessan hin dhufu; ani yoon deeme garuu isa isiniif nan erga.
> *Nevertheless I tell you the truth: It is to your advantage that I go away; for if I don’t go away, the Counselor won’t come to you. But if I go, I will send him to you.*
**8.** Innis yommuu dhufutti akka addunyaan waaʼee cubbuu, waaʼee qajeelummaatii fi waaʼee murtii dogoggoree jiru mirkaneessa;
> *When he has come, he will convict the world about sin, about righteousness, and about judgment;*
**9.** waaʼee cubbuu, sababii namoonni natti hin amanneef;
> *about sin, because they don’t believe in me;*
**10.** waaʼee qajeelummaa, sababii ani gara Abbaa dhaquu fi sababii isinis siʼachi na hin argineef;
> *about righteousness, because I am going to my Father, and you won’t see me any more;*
**11.** waaʼee murtii immoo sababii bulchaa addunyaa kanaatti murameef.
> *about judgment, because the prince of this world has been judged.*
**12.** “Ani amma iyyuu waanan isinitti himu baayʼee qaba; isin garuu amma baachuu hin dandeessan.
> *“I still have many things to tell you, but you can’t bear them now.*
**13.** Hafuurri dhugaa sun yommuu dhufutti gara dhugaa hundaatti isin geessa. Inni waan dhagaʼu qofa dubbata malee ofuma isaatiin hin dubbatu; waan dhufuuf jirus isinitti hima.
> *However, when he, the Spirit of truth, has come, he will guide you into all truth, for he will not speak from himself; but whatever he hears, he will speak. He will declare to you things that are coming.*
**14.** Inni sababii waanuma koo fuudhee isinitti beeksisuuf ulfina naa kenna.
> *He will glorify me, for he will take from what is mine and will declare it to you.*
**15.** Wanni Abbaan qabu hundinuu kan koo ti. Inni waanuma koo fuudhee isinitti beeksisa jechuun koos kanumaaf.
> *All things that the Father has are mine; therefore I said that he takes of mine and will declare it to you.*
**16.** “Isin yeroo muraasa booddee na hin argitan; yeroo muraasa booddee immoo deebitanii na argitu.”
> *“A little while, and you will not see me. Again a little while, and you will see me.”*
**17.** Barattoota isaa keessaas tokko tokko, “Inni, ‘Isin yeroo muraasaaf na hin argitan; yeroo muraasaaf immoo deebitanii na argitu’ akkanumas ‘Ani waanan gara Abbaa dhaquuf’ nuun jechuun isaa maal jechuu isaa ti?” waliin jedhan.
> *Some of his disciples therefore said to one another, “What is this that he says to us, ‘A little while, and you won’t see me, and again a little while, and you will see me;’ and, ‘Because I go to the Father’?”*
**18.** Isaanis, “ ‘Yeroo muraasaaf’ jechuun isaa maal jechuu isaa ti? Maal jechuu isaa akka taʼe nu hin beeknu” jedhan.
> *They said therefore, “What is this that he says, ‘A little while’? We don’t know what he is saying.”*
**19.** Yesuus immoo akka isaan isa gaafachuu barbaadan beekee akkana jedheen; “Wanni ani, ‘Isin yeroo muraasaaf na hin argitan; yeroo muraasaaf immoo deebitanii na argitu’ jedhe sun maal jechuu koo akka taʼe wal gaafachaa jirtuu?
> *Therefore Jesus perceived that they wanted to ask him, and he said to them, “Do you inquire among yourselves concerning this, that I said, ‘A little while, and you won’t see me, and again a little while, and you will see me’?*
**20.** Ani dhuguman, dhuguman isinitti hima; isin ni boossu; ni gadittus. Addunyaan garuu ni gammada. Isin ni gadditu; gaddi keessan garuu gammachuutti ni geeddarama.
> *Most certainly I tell you that you will weep and lament, but the world will rejoice. You will be sorrowful, but your sorrow will be turned into joy.*
**21.** Dubartiin yommuu ciniinsifattu, waan yeroon ishee gaʼeef ni gadditi; erga daaʼima deessee booddee garuu waan addunyaa irratti namni dhalateef, isheenis waan gammadduuf dhiphina sana hin yaadattu.
> *A woman, when she gives birth, has sorrow because her time has come. But when she has delivered the child, she doesn’t remember the anguish any more, for the joy that a human being is born into the world.*
**22.** Isinis akkasuma amma gadditaniirtu; ani garuu deebiʼeen isin ilaala; garaan keessanis ni gammada. Gammachuu keessanis eenyu iyyuu isin irraa hin fudhatu.
> *Therefore you now have sorrow, but I will see you again, and your heart will rejoice, and no one will take your joy away from you.*
**23.** Gaafas isin homaa na hin kadhattan. Ani dhuguman, dhuguman isinitti hima; Abbaan waan isin maqaa kootiin kadhattan kam iyyuu isinii kenna.
> *“In that day you will ask me no questions. Most certainly I tell you, whatever you may ask of the Father in my name, he will give it to you.*
**24.** Isin hamma ammaatti maqaa kootiin homaa hin kadhanne. Akka gammachuun keessan guutuu taʼuuf kadhadhaa ni argattu.
> *Until now, you have asked nothing in my name. Ask, and you will receive, that your joy may be made full.*
**25.** “Ani wantoota kanneen fakkeenyaan isinitti himeera; garuu yeroon ani deebiʼee fakkeenyaan isinitti hin dubbanne ni dhufa; yommus ani waaʼee Abbaa koo ifaan ifatti isinittin hima.
> *“I have spoken these things to you in figures of speech. But the time is coming when I will no more speak to you in figures of speech, but will tell you plainly about the Father.*
**26.** Isin gaafas maqaa kootiin ni kadhattu. Ani Abbaa isiniif nan kadhadha isiniin hin jedhu.
> *In that day you will ask in my name; and I don’t say to you that I will pray to the Father for you,*
**27.** Isin waan na jaallattaniif, Waaqa biraa baʼuu koo waan amantaniif Abbaan mataan isaa isin jaallata.
> *for the Father himself loves you, because you have loved me, and have believed that I came from God.*
**28.** Ani Abbaa biraa baʼee gara addunyaa dhufeera; ammas addunyaa dhiisee gara Abbaa nan dhaqa.”
> *I came from the Father and have come into the world. Again, I leave the world and go to the Father.”*
**29.** Barattoonni isaas akkana jedhan; “Kunoo, ati amma iyyuu fakkeenyaan utuu hin taʼin ifaan ifatti dubbachaa jirta.
> *His disciples said to him, “Behold, now you are speaking plainly, and using no figures of speech.*
**30.** Akka ati waan hunda beektuu fi akka namni tokko iyyuu gaaffii si gaafachuu hin barbaachifne nu amma ni beekna. Kanaanis nu akka ati Waaqa biraa baate ni amanna.”
> *Now we know that you know all things, and don’t need for anyone to question you. By this we believe that you came from God.”*
**31.** Yesuusis deebisee akkana jedheen; “Isin amma ni amantuu?
> *Jesus answered them, “Do you now believe?*
**32.** Kunoo yeroon isin itti kophaatti na dhiiftanii tokkoon tokkoon keessan gara feetanitti bittinnooftan tokko ni dhufa. Ammuma iyyuu dhufeera. Garuu waan Abbaan koo na wajjin jiruuf ani kophaa koo miti.
> *Behold, the time is coming, yes, and has now come, that you will be scattered, everyone to his own place, and you will leave me alone. Yet I am not alone, because the Father is with me.*
**33.** “Akka isin anaan nagaa qabaattaniif ani wantoota kanneen isinitti himeera. Addunyaa kana irratti rakkinni isin argata. Garuu jabaadhaa! Ani addunyaa moʼadheeraa.”
> *I have told you these things, that in me you may have peace. In the world you have trouble; but cheer up! I have overcome the world.”*

## Boqonnaa 17 / Chapter 17

**1.** Yesuus wantoota kanneen dubbatee gara samii ol ilaalee akkana jedhe: “Yaa Abbaa, saʼaatiin sun gaʼeera. Akka Ilmi kees ulfina siif kennuuf, ati Ilma keetiif ulfina kenni.
> *Jesus said these things, then lifting up his eyes to heaven, he said, “Father, the time has come. Glorify your Son, that your Son may also glorify you;*
**2.** Akka inni warra ati isaaf kennite hundaaf jireenya bara baraa kennuuf, nama hunda irratti taayitaa isaaf kenniteertaatii.
> *even as you gave him authority over all flesh, so he will give eternal life to all whom you have given him.*
**3.** Jireenyi bara baraas kana: innis akka isaan siʼi Waaqa dhugaa tokkichaa fi Yesuus Kiristoos isa ati ergite sana beekaniif.
> *This is eternal life, that they should know you, the only true God, and him whom you sent, Jesus Christ.*
**4.** Ani hojii ati akka ani hojjedhuuf natti kennite sana raawwachuudhaan lafa irratti ulfina siif kenneera.
> *I glorified you on the earth. I have accomplished the work which you have given me to do.*
**5.** Yaa Abbaa, amma immoo ati ulfina ani utuu addunyaan hin uumaminiin dura si biratti qabaadhe sanaan of biratti na ulfeessi.
> *Now, Father, glorify me with your own self with the glory which I had with you before the world existed.*
**6.** “Ani namoota ati addunyaa keessaa naa kennitetti maqaa kee beeksiseera. Isaan kan kee turan; ati isaan naa kennite; isaanis dubbii kee eeganiiru.
> *“I revealed your name to the people whom you have given me out of the world. They were yours, and you have given them to me. They have kept your word.*
**7.** Isaan akka wanni ati anaaf kennite hundumtuu si biraa dhufe amma beekaniiru.
> *Now they have known that all things whatever you have given me are from you,*
**8.** Ani dubbii ati anaaf kennite isaaniif kenneeraatii; isaanis fudhataniiru. Akka ani si biraa baʼes dhugumaan beekaniiru; akka ati na ergites amananiiru.
> *for the words which you have given me I have given to them; and they received them, and knew for sure that I came from you. They have believed that you sent me.*
**9.** Ani isaaniif nan kadhadha. Warri ati naa kennite waan kan kee taʼaniif isaaniifin kadhadha malee addunyaaf hin kadhadhu.
> *I pray for them. I don’t pray for the world, but for those whom you have given me, for they are yours.*
**10.** Wanni ani qabu hundinuu kan kee ti; wanni ati qabdu hundinuus kan koo ti. Anis kanaan ulfina nan argadha.
> *All things that are mine are yours, and yours are mine, and I am glorified in them.*
**11.** Ani siʼachi addunyaa keessa hin turu; isaan garuu amma iyyuu addunyaa keessa jiru; ani gara kee nan dhufa. Yaa Abbaa Qulqulluu, akkuma nu tokko taane sana isaanis tokko akka taʼaniif maqaa kee kan naa kennite sanaan isaan eegi.
> *I am no more in the world, but these are in the world, and I am coming to you. Holy Father, keep them through your name which you have given me, that they may be one, even as we are.*
**12.** Ani yeroo isaan wajjin turetti, maqaa kee kan ati naa kennite sanaan isaan nan eege; isaan eegeeras. Akka Katabbiin Qulqulluun raawwatamuufis ilma badii sana malee isaan keessaa namni tokko iyyuu hin badne.
> *While I was with them in the world, I kept them in your name. I have kept those whom you have given me. None of them is lost except the son of destruction, that the Scripture might be fulfilled.*
**13.** “Amma garuu ani gara kee nan dhufa; gammachuun koos akka isaan keessatti guutuu taʼuuf, ani amma iyyuu utuman addunyaa keessa jiruu wantoota kanneen nan dubbadha.
> *But now I come to you, and I say these things in the world, that they may have my joy made full in themselves.*
**14.** Ani dubbii kee isaaniif kenneera; akkuma ani kan addunyaa hin taʼin, isaanis waan kan addunyaa hin taʼiniif, addunyaan isaan jibbeera.
> *I have given them your word. The world hated them because they are not of the world, even as I am not of the world.*
**15.** Ani akka ati isa hamaa sana isaan irraa eegduuf malee akka ati addunyaa keessaa isaan baaftuuf si hin kadhadhu.
> *I pray not that you would take them from the world, but that you would keep them from the evil one.*
**16.** Akkuma ani kan addunyaa hin taʼin isaanis akkasuma kan addunyaa miti.
> *They are not of the world, even as I am not of the world.*
**17.** Dhugaadhaan isaan qulqulleessi; dubbiin kee dhugaa dha.
> *Sanctify them in your truth. Your word is truth.*
**18.** Akkuma ati gara addunyaatti na ergite sana anis gara addunyaatti isaan ergeera.
> *As you sent me into the world, even so I have sent them into the world.*
**19.** Akka isaanis dhugumaan qulqullaaʼaniif, ani isaaniif jedhee of nan qulqulleessa.
> *For their sakes I sanctify myself, that they themselves also may be sanctified in truth.*
**20.** “Ani warra dubbii isaaniitiin natti amananiifis malee isaan kana qofaaf hin kadhadhu;
> *“Not for these only do I pray, but for those also who will believe in me through their word,*
**21.** kunis yaa Abbaa, akkuma ati na keessa jirtu, anis si keessa jiru sana, akka hundi isaanii tokko taʼaniif. Akka ati na ergite addunyaan akka amanuuf isaanis nu keessa haa jiraatan.
> *that they may all be one; even as you, Father, are in me, and I in you, that they also may be one in us; that the world may believe that you sent me.*
**22.** Akkuma nu tokko taane isaanis tokko akka taʼaniif, ulfina ati naaf kennite sana anis isaaniif kenneera;
> *The glory which you have given me, I have given to them, that they may be one, even as we are one,*
**23.** kunis akka isaan guutummaatti tokko taʼaniif ani isaan keessa nan jiraadha; ati immoo na keessa jiraatta. Yoos akka ati na ergitee fi akkuma na jaallattetti akka isaanis jaallatte addunyaan ni beeka.
> *I in them, and you in me, that they may be perfected into one, that the world may know that you sent me and loved them, even as you loved me.*
**24.** “Yaa Abbaa, akka warri ati naa kennite kunneen iddoo ani jiru na wajjin jiraatanii fi akka isaan ulfina koo kan ati utuu addunyaan hin uumaminiin dura waan na jaallatteef naa kennite sana arganis nan barbaada.
> *Father, I desire that they also whom you have given me be with me where I am, that they may see my glory which you have given me, for you loved me before the foundation of the world.*
**25.** “Yaa Abbaa Qajeelticha, addunyaan si hin beeku; ani garuu sin beeka; isaan kunis akka ati na ergite ni beeku.
> *Righteous Father, the world hasn’t known you, but I knew you; and these knew that you sent me.*
**26.** Akkan jaalalli ati ittiin na jaallatte sun isaan keessa jiraatuuf, anis akka isaan keessa jiraadhuuf ani akka maqaan kee isaan biratti beekamu godheera; akka beekamus nan godha.”
> *I made known to them your name, and will make it known; that the love with which you loved me may be in them, and I in them.”*

## Boqonnaa 18 / Chapter 18

**1.** Yesuus wantoota kanneen dubbatee barattoota isaa wajjin Sulula Qeediroon gama iddoo biqiltuun ture tokkotti ceʼe; innis, barattoonni isaas achi keessa seenan.
> *When Jesus had spoken these words, he went out with his disciples over the brook Kidron, where there was a garden, into which he and his disciples entered.*
**2.** Yesuus yeroo baayʼee barattoota isaa wajjin achitti wal argaa waan tureef, Yihuudaan inni Yesuusin dabarsee kennu sunis iddoo sana beeka ture.
> *Now Judas, who betrayed him, also knew the place, for Jesus often met there with his disciples.*
**3.** Kanaafuu Yihuudaan tuuta loltootaatii fi qondaaltota luboota hangafootaa fi Fariisota biraa ergaman dura deemaa achi dhufe. Isaanis guca, ibsaa fi miʼa lolaa qabatanii turan.
> *Judas then, having taken a detachment of soldiers and officers from the chief priests and the Pharisees, came there with lanterns, torches, and weapons.*
**4.** Yesuusis waan isa irra gaʼuuf jiru hunda beeknaan gad baʼee, “Isin eenyun barbaaddu?” jedhee isaan gaafate.
> *Jesus therefore, knowing all the things that were happening to him, went out and said to them, “Who are you looking for?”*
**5.** Isaanis deebisanii, “Yesuus isa Naazreeti” jedhan. Yesuus immoo, “Ani isaa dha” jedhe. Yihuudaan inni dabarsee isa kennu sunis jara wajjin achi dhaabachaa ture.

## Boqonnaa 20 / Chapter 20

**1.** Guyyaa jalqaba torban sanaa ganama barii utuma dukkanaaʼee jiruu Maariyaam isheen Magdalaa gara awwaalaa dhaqxee akka dhagaan afaan awwaala sanaa irraa fuudhame argite.
> *Now on the first day of the week, Mary Magdalene went early, while it was still dark, to the tomb, and saw that the stone had been taken away from the tomb.*
**2.** Kanaafuu isheen fiigaa gara Simoon Phexrosii fi gara barataa biraa kan Yesuus isa jaallate sanaa dhaqxee, “Gooftaa awwaala keessaa fudhataniiru; eessa akka isa kaaʼanis hin beeknu!” jetteen.
> *Therefore she ran and came to Simon Peter and to the other disciple whom Jesus loved, and said to them, “They have taken away the Lord out of the tomb, and we don’t know where they have laid him!”*
**3.** Kanaafuu Phexrosii fi barataan kaan baʼanii gara awwaalichaa dhaqan.
> *Therefore Peter and the other disciple went out, and they went toward the tomb.*
**4.** Lachan isaanii iyyuu ni fiigu turan; barataan kaan garuu Phexros bira darbee fiiguudhaan dursee iddoo awwaalaa sana gaʼe.
> *They both ran together. The other disciple outran Peter and came to the tomb first.*
**5.** Innis gad jedhee ilaalee huccuu quncee talbaa irraa hojjetame lafatti arge malee awwaalicha keessa hin seenne.
> *Stooping and looking in, he saw the linen cloths lying there; yet he didn’t enter in.*
**6.** Simoon Phexros immoo isa duubaan dhufee awwaalicha keessa seene. Huccuu sanas lafatti arge;
> *Then Simon Peter came, following him, and entered into the tomb. He saw the linen cloths lying,*
**7.** akkanumas huccuu mataa Yesuusitti maramee ture arge. Huccuun sunis kophaa isaa dachaafamee iddoo biraa ture malee huccuu quncee talbaa irraa hojjetame sana bira hin turre.
> *and the cloth that had been on his head, not lying with the linen cloths, but rolled up in a place by itself.*
**8.** Ergasiis barataan biraa inni jalqabatti awwaalicha bira gaʼe sun keessa seene. Innis argee amane.
> *So then the other disciple who came first to the tomb also entered in, and he saw and believed.*
**9.** Isaan akka Yesuus warra duʼan keessaa kaʼu Katabbii Qulqulluu irraa amma iyyuu hin beekne tureetii.
> *For as yet they didn’t know the Scripture, that he must rise from the dead.*
**10.** Barattoonni sunis gara mana isaaniitti deebiʼan.
> *So the disciples went away again to their own homes.*
**11.** Maariyaam garuu alaan awwaala bira dhaabattee booʼaa turte. Utuma boossuus awwaallatti gad jettee ilaalte;
> *But Mary was standing outside at the tomb weeping. So as she wept, she stooped and looked into the tomb,*
**12.** isheenis ergamoota lama kanneen uffata adaadii uffatanii iddoo reeffi Yesuus kaaʼamee ture sana, inni tokko mataa duraan, kaan immoo miilla jalaan taaʼan argite.
> *and she saw two angels in white sitting, one at the head and one at the feet, where the body of Jesus had lain.*
**13.** Isaanis, “Dubartii nana, maaliif boossa?” jedhaniin. Isheen immoo, “Gooftaa koo fudhataniiru; eessa akka isa kaaʼanis ani hin beeku” jetteen.
> *They asked her, “Woman, why are you weeping?” She said to them, “Because they have taken away my Lord, and I don’t know where they have laid him.”*
**14.** Yommuu waan kana dubbattee of irra gara galtettis Yesuusin isaa achi dhaabatu argite; garuu Yesuus taʼuu isaa hin beekne.
> *When she had said this, she turned around and saw Jesus standing, and didn’t know that it was Jesus.*
**15.** Yesuusis, “Dubartii nana, maaliif boossa? Eenyun barbaadda?” jedheen. Isheen immoo, nama iddoo biqiltuu keessa hojjetu seetee, “Yaa gooftaa, ati yoo isa fuutee jiraatte, akka ani dhaqee fudhadhuuf eessa akka isa keesse natti himi” jetteen.
> *Jesus said to her, “Woman, why are you weeping? Who are you looking for?” She, supposing him to be the gardener, said to him, “Sir, if you have carried him away, tell me where you have laid him, and I will take him away.”*
**16.** Yesuusis, “Maariyaam!” jedheen. Isheenis isatti gara galtee afaan Ibraayisxiitiin, “Rabbuunii!” jetteen. Hiikkaan isaas “Barsiisaa” jechuu dha.
> *Jesus said to her, “Mary.” She turned and said to him, “Rabboni!” which is to say, “Teacher!”*
**17.** Yesuus immoo, “Ani amma illee waan gara Abbaatti ol hin baʼiniif na hin tuqin. Garuu obboloota koo bira dhaqiitii, ‘Ani gara Abbaa kootii fi Abbaa keessaniitti, gara Waaqa kootii fi Waaqa keessaniitti ol nan baʼa’ jedheera jedhiitii isaanitti himi” jedheen.
> *Jesus said to her, “Don’t hold me, for I haven’t yet ascended to my Father; but go to my brothers and tell them, ‘I am ascending to my Father and your Father, to my God and your God.’”*
**18.** Maariyaam isheen Magdalaas gara barattootaa dhaqxee, “Ani Gooftaa argeera!” jette. Akka inni waan kana isheen jedhes isaanitti himte.
> *Mary Magdalene came and told the disciples that she had seen the Lord, and that he had said these things to her.*
**19.** Guyyaa jalqaba torban sanaa galgala utuu barattoonni waan Yihuudoota sodaataniif balbala cufatanii walitti qabamanii jiranuu Yesuus dhufee isaan gidduu dhaabatee, “Nagaan isiniif haa taʼu” jedheen.
> *When therefore it was evening on that day, the first day of the week, and when the doors were locked where the disciples were assembled, for fear of the Jews, Jesus came and stood in the middle and said to them, “Peace be to you.”*
**20.** Innis erga waan kana dubbatee booddee harka isaatii fi cinaacha isaa isaanitti argisiise. Barattoonnis yommuu Gooftaa arganitti akka malee gammadan.
> *When he had said this, he showed them his hands and his side. The disciples therefore were glad when they saw the Lord.*
**21.** Yesuus amma illee, “Nagaan isiniif haa taʼu! Akkuma Abbaan na erge sana anis isinan erga” jedheen.
> *Jesus therefore said to them again, “Peace be to you. As the Father has sent me, even so I send you.”*
**22.** Innis erga waan kana jedhee booddee Hafuura isaanitti baafatee akkana jedheen; “Hafuura Qulqulluu fudhadhaa.
> *When he had said this, he breathed on them, and said to them, “Receive the Holy Spirit!*
**23.** Yoo isin namootaaf cubbuu isaanii dhiiftan isaaniif ni dhiifama; yoo isaaniif dhiisuu baattan immoo isaaniif hin dhiifamu.”
> *If you forgive anyone’s sins, they have been forgiven them. If you retain anyone’s sins, they have been retained.”*
**24.** Garuu yeroo Yesuus dhufetti warra Kudha Lamaan keessaa Toomaas inni Didimoos jedhamu tokko barattoota wajjin hin turre.
> *But Thomas, one of the twelve, called Didymus, wasn’t with them when Jesus came.*
**25.** Kanaafuu barattoonni warri kaan, “Nu Gooftaa argineerra!” jedhanii isatti himan. Inni garuu, “Ani iddoo mismaaraan waraaname harka isaa irratti argu malee, quba koo iddoo mismaaraa sana keessa kaaʼu malee, harka koos cinaacha isaa keessa kaaʼu malee hin amanu” jedheen.
> *The other disciples therefore said to him, “We have seen the Lord!” But he said to them, “Unless I see in his hands the print of the nails, put my finger into the print of the nails, and put my hand into his side, I will not believe.”*
**26.** Guyyaa saddeeti booddee barattoonni isaa ammas mana keessa turan; Toomaasis isaan wajjin ture. Utuma balbalichi cufamee jiruus Yesuus ol galee isaan gidduu dhaabatee, “Nagaan isiniif haa taʼu!” jedheen.
> *After eight days, again his disciples were inside and Thomas was with them. Jesus came, the doors being locked, and stood in the middle, and said, “Peace be to you.”*
**27.** Innis Toomaasiin, “Quba kee as fidi; harka koo ilaali. Harka kee diriirfadhuutii cinaacha koo keessa kaaʼi. Amani malee amantii hin dhabin” jedhe.
> *Then he said to Thomas, “Reach here your finger, and see my hands. Reach here your hand, and put it into my side. Don’t be unbelieving, but believing.”*
**28.** Toomaas immoo, “Gooftaa koo! Waaqa koo!” jedheen.
> *Thomas answered him, “My Lord and my God!”*
**29.** Yesuusis, “Ati waan na argiteef amante; warri utuu hin argin amanan eebbifamoo dha” jedheen.
> *Jesus said to him, “Because you have seen me, you have believed. Blessed are those who have not seen and have believed.”*
**30.** Yesuus barattoota isaa duratti mallattoowwan biraa kanneen kitaaba kana keessatti hin barreeffamin hedduu hojjete.
> *Therefore Jesus did many other signs in the presence of his disciples, which are not written in this book;*
**31.** Wantoonni kunneen garuu akka Yesuus inni Kiristoos Ilma Waaqaa taʼe akka amantaniif, amanuudhaanis akka maqaa isaatiin jireenya qabaattaniif barreeffame.
> *but these are written that you may believe that Jesus is the Christ, the Son of God, and that believing you may have life in his name.*

## Boqonnaa 21 / Chapter 21

**1.** Wantoota kanneen booddee Yesuus ammas galaana Xibeeriyaas biratti barattoota isaatti of mulʼise. Haalli inni ittiin of mulʼises akkana ture:
> *After these things, Jesus revealed himself again to the disciples at the sea of Tiberias. He revealed himself this way.*
**2.** Simoon Phexros, Toomaas inni Didimoos jedhamu, Naatnaaʼel namichi Qaanaa Galiilaa, ilmaan Zabdewoosii fi barattoonni biraa lama walii wajjin turan.
> *Simon Peter, Thomas called Didymus, Nathanael of Cana in Galilee, and the sons of Zebedee, and two others of his disciples were together.*
**3.** Simoon Phexrosis, “Ani qurxummii qabuu nan dhaqa” isaaniin jedhe; isaanis, “Nus si wajjin dhaqna” jedhaniin. Gad baʼaniis bidiruu yaabbatan; halkan sana garuu homaa hin qabne.
> *Simon Peter said to them, “I’m going fishing.” They told him, “We are also coming with you.” They immediately went out and entered into the boat. That night, they caught nothing.*
**4.** Ganama bariidhaanis Yesuus qarqara galaanichaa irra dhaabachaa ture; barattoonni garuu akka inni Yesuus taʼe hin beekne.
> *But when day had already come, Jesus stood on the beach; yet the disciples didn’t know that it was Jesus.*
**5.** Innis isaan waamee, “Ijoolle, qurxummii tokko illee hin qabdanii?” jedhee gaafate. Isaan immoo, “Hin qabnu” jedhanii deebisan.
> *Jesus therefore said to them, “Children, have you anything to eat?” They answered him, “No.”*
**6.** Innis, “Karaa mirga bidiruutiin kiyyoo gad darbadhaa; qurxummii ni argattuu” jedheen. Kanaafuu isaan kiyyoo sana gad darbatan; sababii baayʼina qurxummiitiifis kiyyoo sana harkisuu dadhaban.
> *He said to them, “Cast the net on the right side of the boat, and you will find some.” They cast it therefore, and now they weren’t able to draw it in for the multitude of fish.*
**7.** Barataan inni Yesuus isa jaallachaa ture sunis, “Inni Gooftaa dha!” jedhee Phexrositti hime. Kanaafuu Simoon Phexros yommuu “Inni Gooftaa dha” jechuu isaa dhagaʼetti waan marxoo isaa of irraa baasee tureef, marxoo isaa marxifatee bishaanitti gad utaale.
> *That disciple therefore whom Jesus loved said to Peter, “It’s the Lord!” So when Simon Peter heard that it was the Lord, he wrapped his coat around himself (for he was naked), and threw himself into the sea.*
**8.** Barattoonni kaan garuu waan qarqara galaanaa irraa dhundhuma dhibba lama caalaa hin fagaatiniif kiyyoo qurxummiin keessa guute sana harkisaa bidiruudhaan dhufan.
> *But the other disciples came in the little boat (for they were not far from the land, but about two hundred cubits away), dragging the net full of fish.*
**9.** Yommuu lafatti baʼanittis ibidda cilee kan qurxummiin irra jiruu fi buddeena achitti argan.
> *So when they got out on the land, they saw a fire of coals there, with fish and bread laid on it.*
**10.** Yesuusis, “Mee qurxummii amma qabdan irraa waa fidaa!” isaaniin jedhe.
> *Jesus said to them, “Bring some of the fish which you have just caught.”*
**11.** Simoon Phexrosis bidiruu sana yaabbatee kiyyoo qurxummiin gurguddaan 153 guute sana gara lafaatti harkise. Kiyyoon sunis qurxummii akkas baayʼattuun guutamu illee hin tarsaane.
> *Simon Peter went up, and drew the net to land, full of one hundred fifty-three great fish. Even though there were so many, the net wasn’t torn.*
**12.** Yesuusis, “Kottaa ciree nyaadhaa!” isaaniin jedhe. Barattoota keessaas namni ija jabaatee, “Ati eenyu?” jedhee isa gaafate tokko iyyuu hin turre. Isaan akka inni Gooftaa taʼe beekaniiruutii.
> *Jesus said to them, “Come and eat breakfast!” None of the disciples dared inquire of him, “Who are you?” knowing that it was the Lord.*
**13.** Yesuusis dhufee buddeena sana fuudhee isaaniif kenne; qurxummichas akkasuma godhe.
> *Then Jesus came and took the bread, gave it to them, and the fish likewise.*
**14.** Egaa Yesuus erga duʼaa kaafamee, kana wajjin yeroo sadaffaa barattoota isaatti mulʼate.
> *This is now the third time that Jesus was revealed to his disciples after he had risen from the dead.*
**15.** Yesuus erga isaan ciree nyaatanii booddee Simʼoon Phexrosiin, “Yaa Simoon ilma Yohannis, ati warra kana caalaa na jaallattaa?” jedhe. Innis, “Eeyyee, yaa Gooftaa; akka ani si jaalladhu atuu ni beekta” jedheen. Yesuusis, “Ilmoolee hoolaa koo soori” jedheen.
> *So when they had eaten their breakfast, Jesus said to Simon Peter, “Simon, son of Jonah, do you love me more than these?” He said to him, “Yes, Lord; you know that I have affection for you.” He said to him, “Feed my lambs.”*
**16.** Yesuus amma illee, “Yaa Simoon ilma Yohannis, ati na jaallattaa?” jedheen. Innis deebisee, “Eeyyee, yaa Gooftaa, akka ani si jaalladhu atuu ni beekta” jedheen. Yesuus immoo, “Hoolota koo tiksi” jedheen.
> *He said to him again a second time, “Simon, son of Jonah, do you love me?” He said to him, “Yes, Lord; you know that I have affection for you.” He said to him, “Tend my sheep.”*
**17.** Yeroo sadaffaas, “Yaa Simoon ilma Yohannis, ati na jaallattaa?” jedheen. Phexrosis sababii Yesuus yeroo sadaffaa, “Ati na jaallattaa?” jedhee isa gaafateef gaddee, “Yaa Gooftaa, ati waan hunda beekta; akka ani si jaalladhus ni beekta” jedheen. Yesuusis akkana jedheen; “Hoolota koo soori.
> *He said to him the third time, “Simon, son of Jonah, do you have affection for me?” Peter was grieved because he asked him the third time, “Do you have affection for me?” He said to him, “Lord, you know everything. You know that I have affection for you.” Jesus said to him, “Feed my sheep.*
**18.** Ani dhuguman, dhuguman sitti hima; ati yeroo dargaggeessa turtetti, ofuma keetiin mudhii kee hidhattee lafa barbaadde dhaqxa turte; yeroo dulloomtutti garuu harka kee ni diriirfatta; namni biraas mudhii kee siif hidhee lafa ati hin barbaannetti si geessa.”
> *Most certainly I tell you, when you were young, you dressed yourself and walked where you wanted to. But when you are old, you will stretch out your hands, and another will dress you and carry you where you don’t want to go.”*
**19.** Yesuusis akka Phexros duʼa akkamiitiin Waaqa ulfeessu argisiisuuf jedhee waan kana dubbate; ergasii, “Na duukaa buʼi” jedheen.
> *Now he said this, signifying by what kind of death he would glorify God. When he had said this, he said to him, “Follow me.”*
**20.** Phexrosis garagalee utuu barataan inni Yesuus isa jaallatu sun isaan duukaa buʼuu arge. Barataan kunis isa yeroo irbaataatti qoma Yesuusiitti irkatee, “Yaa Gooftaa, inni dabarsee si kennu eenyu?” jedhe sanaa dha.
> *Then Peter, turning around, saw a disciple following. This was the disciple whom Jesus loved, the one who had also leaned on Jesus’ chest at the supper and asked, “Lord, who is going to betray you?”*
**21.** Phexrosis yommuu isa argetti, “Yaa Gooftaa, namichi kun hoo?” jedhee Yesuusin gaafate.
> *Peter, seeing him, said to Jesus, “Lord, what about this man?”*
**22.** Yesuusis deebisee, “Hamma ani dhufutti akka inni jiraatu yoo fedhii koo taʼe, maaltu si dhibe? Ati na duukaa buʼi” jedheen.
> *Jesus said to him, “If I desire that he stay until I come, what is that to you? You follow me.”*
**23.** Kanaaf oduun, “Barataan kun hin duʼu” jedhu obboloota gidduutti odeeffame. Yesuus garuu, “Hamma ani dhufutti akka inni jiraatu yoo fedhii koo taʼe maaltu si dhibe?” jedhe malee, “Inni hin duʼu” hin jenne.
> *This saying therefore went out among the brothers that this disciple wouldn’t die. Yet Jesus didn’t say to him that he wouldn’t die, but, “If I desire that he stay until I come, what is that to you?”*
**24.** Inni dubbii kana dhugaa baʼu, kan waan kanas barreesse barataadhuma kana. Akka dhuga baʼumsi isaa dhugaa taʼes nu beekna.
> *This is the disciple who testifies about these things, and wrote these things. We know that his witness is true.*
**25.** Kana malees Yesuus waan biraa baayʼee hojjeteera. Utuu tokkoon tokkoon isaanii barreeffamanii jiraatanii silaa ani waan addunyaan mataan ishee iyyuu kitaabota barreeffaman sanaaf iddoo gaʼu qabdu hin seʼu.
> *There are also many other things which Jesus did, which if they would all be written, I suppose that even the world itself wouldn’t have room for the books that would be written.*
